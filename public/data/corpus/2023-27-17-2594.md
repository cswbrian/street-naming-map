## English

G.N. 2594 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of the following road in the Kowloon City District, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. The road is approximately 135 metres long. It starts from its junction with Olympic Avenue, and ends at its junction with Muk Wo Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured blue on Plan No. KRM140. DAKOTA DRIVE G.N. 3826 dated 13 November 1987 G.N. 308 dated 29 January 1988 A copy of Plan No. KRM140 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 3rd Floor, South Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon; in the Map Publications Centre, Hong Kong at 6th Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 28 April 2023 CHU Siu-ki for Director of Lands
