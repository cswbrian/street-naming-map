## English

G.N. 7995 Lands Department STREET NAMES Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following roads in Kwai Tsing District, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 295 metres long. It is an extension of the existing Cheung Tsing Highway. It starts from its junction with Tsing Sha Highway and merges with the existing Cheung Tsing Highway. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. KTRM70b. CHEUNG TSING HIGHWAY The road is approximately 4 600 metres long. It is an extension of the existing Tsing Sha Highway. It starts from its junction with Cheung Tsing Highway and another junction with Tsing Yi Road. It ends where it joins the existing Tsing Sha Highway at Stonecutters Island (Ngong Shuen Chau). Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. KTRM72c. TSING SHA HIGHWAY The road is approximately 170 metres long. It extends from the existing Tsing Yi Hong Wan Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. KTRM73c. TSING YI HONG WAN ROAD The road is approximately 900 metres long. It extends from the existing Container Port Road South and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. KTRM74c. CONTAINER PORT ROAD SOUTH Plan Nos. KTRM70b, KTRM72c, KTRM73c and KTRM74c may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Tsuen Wan and Kwai Tsing at 15th Floor, Tsuen Wan Government Offices, 38 Sai Lau Kok Road, Tsuen Wan, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 18 December 2009 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands

## 中文

第 7995 號公告 地政總署 街道命名 現根據 《公眾衞生及市政條例》 ( 第 132 章 ) 第 111C(1)( a ) 條公布，下列位於新界葵青區的道路， 由本公告日期起，採用以下名稱： 說明 名稱 這道路長約 295 米，是現時長青公路的延展部分，以其與青沙公路 的 交 界 處 為 起 點，並 與 現 時 的 長 青 公 路 匯 合。位 置 及 路 線 在 第 KTRM70b 號圖則上以粉紅色標明。 長青公路 這道路長約 4 600 米，是現時青沙公路的延展部分，以其與長青公 路的交界處及其與青衣路的另一交界處為起點，至其於昂船洲與現 時的青沙公路連接之處終結。位置及路線在第 KTRM72c 號圖則上 以粉紅色標明。 青沙公路 這道路長約 170 米，由現有青衣航運路延伸出來，至其盡頭處終結。 位置及路線在第 KTRM73c 號圖則上以黑網點標明。 青衣航運路 這道路長約 900 米，由現有貨櫃碼頭南路延伸出來，至其盡頭處終 結。位置及路線在第 KTRM74c 號圖則上以粉紅色標明。 貨櫃碼頭南路 查閱第 KTRM70b 、 KTRM72c 、 KTRM73c 及 KTRM74c 號圖則，可到新界荃灣西樓角路 38 號荃灣政府合署 15 樓荃灣葵青測量處、香港北角渣華道 333 號北角政府合署 23 樓地政總署 測繪處，或香港銅鑼灣高士威道 66 號香港中央圖書館 5 樓地圖圖書館。 2009 年 12 月 18 日 地政總署署長 ( 邵偉青代行 )
