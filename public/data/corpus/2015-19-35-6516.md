## English

G.N. 6516 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following streets in the district of Kwun Tong, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 390 metres long. It starts from its junction with On Sau Road. It ends at another junction with On Sau Road about 440 metres south-east of the first junction. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured blue on Plan No. KRM80. The street is approximately 780 metres long. It starts from its junction with On Sau Road. It ends at another junction with On Sau Road about 160 metres south of the first junction. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM80. ON CHUI STREET ON YAN STREET A copy of Plan No. KRM80 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 28 August 2015 NG Kwok-wai for Director of Lands
