## English

G.N. 683 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of TUNG CHUNG ROAD in the district of Islands, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace tht set out in G.N. 2429 dated 5 December 1969 with effect from 6 February 2009:— Description Name The road is approximately 7 250 metres long. It starts from its junction with South Lantau Road and ends at a cul-de-sac near Ma Wan New Village in Tung Chung. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured red on Plan No. ISRM75f. TUNG CHUNG ROAD Plan No. ISRM75f may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Islands at 18th Floor, Harbour Building, 38 Pier Road, Central, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong and in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 30 January 2009 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
