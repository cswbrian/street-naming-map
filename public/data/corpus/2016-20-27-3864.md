## English

G.N. 3864 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Sham Shui Po, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 960 metres long. It starts from its junction with Lung Cheung Road at the south and ends at two junctions with Lung Cheung Road at the north. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM87. LUNG KUI ROAD A copy of Plan No. KRM87 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 8 July 2016 LEUNG Kin-wah for Director of Lands
