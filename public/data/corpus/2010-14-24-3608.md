## English

G.N. 3608 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in the district of Tai Po, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 100 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ma Wo Road and ends at a cul-de-sac at the southwest. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. TPRM106. MA SHING PATH Plan No. TPRM106 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Tai Po at 5th Floor, Tai Po Government Offices, 1 Ting Kok Road, Tai Po, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 18 June 2010 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
