## English

G.N. 2232 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in Sai Kung District, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 530 metres long. It starts from its junction with Wan Po Road and ends at its roundabout joining an unnamed road leading to a bus terminus. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. SKRM147a. LOHAS PARK ROAD Plan No. SKRM147a may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Sai Kung at 3rd Floor, Sai Kung Government Offices, 34 Chan Man Street, Sai Kung, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 9 April 2009 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
