## English

G.N. 166 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following roads in the district of Yuen Long, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 880 metres long. It starts from its junction with Lok Ma Chau Road and ends before the fence of Frontier Closed Area near Ha Wan Tsuen. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. YLRM171. HA WAN TSUEN EAST ROAD The road is approximately 235 metres long. It starts from its junction with Lok Ma Chau Road and ends at its junction with Ha Wan Tsuen East Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured blue on Plan No. YLRM171. HA WAN TSUEN ROAD A copy of Plan No. YLRM171 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Yuen Long at 8th Floor, Yuen Long Government Offices, 2 Kiu Lok Square, Yuen Long, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 2 January 2015 NG Kwok-wai for Director of Lands
