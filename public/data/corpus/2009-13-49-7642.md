## English

G.N. 7642 Lands Department STREET NAMES Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following streets in Central and Western District, Hong Kong Island as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 610 metres long. It starts from its junction with Man Yiu Street and ends at its junction with Tim Wa Avenue. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. HKRM31a. The street is approximately 33 metres long. It is an extension of the existing Edinburgh Place. It starts near No. 3 Edinburgh Place and ends at its junction with Lung Wo Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown hatched black on Plan No. HKRM31a. LUNG WO ROAD EDINBURGH PLACE Plan No. HKRM31a may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Hong Kong at 18th Floor, Southorn Centre, 130–150 Hennessy Road, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 4 December 2009 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
