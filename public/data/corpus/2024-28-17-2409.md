## English

G.N. 2409 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the Islands District, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 57 metres long. It starts from its junction with Amalfi Drive and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. ISRM105. TOSCANA DRIVE A copy of Plan No. ISRM105 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the Map Publications Centre, Hong Kong at 6th Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 26 April 2024 CHU Siu-ki for Director of Lands
