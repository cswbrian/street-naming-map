## English

G.N. 5399 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of TIN YAN ROAD in the district of Yuen Long, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 697 dated 28 February 1992 and G.N. 2632 dated 29 May 1998 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 730 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tin Wing Road and ends at its junction with Tin Shing Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. YLRM177a. TIN YAN ROAD A copy of Plan No. YLRM177a and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Yuen Long at 8th Floor, Yuen Long Government Offices, 2 Kiu Lok Square, Yuen Long, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 23 September 2016 LEUNG Kin-wah for Director of Lands
