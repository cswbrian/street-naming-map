| ROAD TRAFFIC (ROADS AND SIGNS) REGULATIONS (Chapter 220)
RESTRICTED ZONE IN RUMSEY STREET
In exercise of the powers vested in me under regulation 18A(1)(b) of the Road Traffic (Roads and
Signs) Regulations, Chapter 220, I hereby direct that with effect from 10.00 a.m. on 25 June 1984,
the western carriageway lane of Rumsey Street between its junction with Des Voeux Road Central
and a point approximately 25 metres south of its junction with Connaught Road Central will be
designated a restricted zone from 7.00 a.m. to 7.00 p.m. daily.
Within the restricted zone, all motor vehicles except franchised buses and those authorized in
writing by the Authority will be prohibited from:—
(a) picking up or setting down passengers; or
(6) loading or unloading goods.
The extent of the restricted zone will be indicated by appropriate traffic signs and carriageway
markings.
The restricted zone on the western carriageway lane of Rumsey Street between its junctions with
Des Voeux Road Central and Connaught Road Central during the hours of 7.00 a.m. to 10.00 a.m.
and from 4.00 p.m. to 7.00 p.m. daily as gazetted in G.N. 1026 of 1982 is hereby rescinded.
P. F. LEEDS Commissioner for Transport
