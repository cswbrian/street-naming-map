## English

G.N. 8863 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the descriptions of MA ON SHAN ROAD and TATE’S CAIRN HIGHWAY in the district of Sha Tin, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 412 dated 31 January 1992 and G.N. 410 dated 31 January 1992 respectively with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 3 350 metres long. It starts from its junction with Sai Sha Road and ends at its junction with Tate’s Cairn Highway, with its slip road joining Hang Tai Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured yellow on Plan No. STRM1162c. MA ON SHAN ROAD The road is approximately 5 050 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tate’s Cairn Tunnel and ends at its junction with Tolo Highway, with its slip roads joining Sha Tin Wai Road, Siu Lek Yuen Road, Ma On Shan Road, Sui Cheung Street and an unnamed road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. STRM1162c. TATE’S CAIRN HIGHWAY A copy of Plan No. STRM1162c and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Sha Tin at 12th Floor, Sha Tin Government Offices, 1 Sheung Wo Che Road, Sha Tin, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 13 November 2015 KWOK Chi-wo for Director of Lands
