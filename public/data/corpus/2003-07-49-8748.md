## English

G.N. 8748 LANDS DEPARTMENT STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Kwai Tsing, New Territories will be known as from the date of this notice as described hereunder:— Description Name TSING YI HONG WAN ROAD Plan No. KTRM63 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Kwai Tsing at 10th Floor, Tsuen Wan Station Multi-storey Carpark Building, 174–208 Castle Peak Road, Tsuen Wan, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong and in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 5 December 2003 P. K. AU YEUNG for Director of Lands The road is approximately 1 350 metres long. It starts at the roundabout with Tsing Yi Road and ends at the cul-de-sac. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. KTRM63.

## 中文

第 8748 號公告 地政總署 街道命名 現根據 《公眾宪生及市政條例》 ( 第 132 章 ) 第 111C(1)( a ) 條公布，下列位於新界葵青區的道路， 由本公告日期起採用以下名稱： 說明 名稱 青衣航運路 查閱第 KTRM63 號圖則可到新界荃灣青山公路 174 至 208 號荃灣地鐵站停車場大廈 10 樓葵 青測量處、香港北角渣華道 333 號北角政府合署 23 樓地政總署測繪處，或香港銅鑼灣高士威道 66 號香港中央圖書館 5 樓地圖圖書館。 2003 年 12 月 5 日 地政總署署長 ( 歐陽炳光代行 ) 這道路長約 1 350 米，以其與青衣路的迴旋處為起點，至其盡頭 處終結。位置及路線在第 KTRM63 號圖則上以粉紅色標明。
