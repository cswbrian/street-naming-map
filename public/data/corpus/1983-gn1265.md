GAZETTE NO. 16/1983 1973
G.N. 1262 TRANSPORT DEPARTMENT
ROAD TRAFFIC (ROADS AND SIGNS) REGULATIONS (Chapter 220)
In exercise of the powers vested in me under regulation 18A(1)(b) of the Road Traffic (Roads and
Signs) Regulations, Chapter 220, I hereby direct that with effect from 10.00 a.m. on 27 April 1983,
the section of the eastbound carriageway of Des Voeux Road West between Hill Road and Whitty
Street will be designated as a restricted zone during the periods from 7.00 a.m. to 10.00 a.m. and 4.00
p.m. to 7.00 p.m. daily.
Within the. restricted zone, drivers of all motor vehicles except franchised buses and those
authorized in writing by the Authority will be prohibited from:—
(a) picking up/setting down passengers;
(b) loading/unloading goods.
The extent of the restricted zone will be indicated by the appropriate traffic signs and carriageway
markings.
P. F. LEEDS Commissioner for Transport
G.N. 1263 TRANSPORT. DEPARTMENT
ROAD TRAFFIC (ROADS AND SIGNS) REGULATIONS (Chapter 220)
In exercise of the powers vested in me under regulation 18A(1)(b) of the Road Traffic (Roads and
Signs) Regulations, Chapter 220, I hereby direct that with effect from 7.00 a.m. on 27 April 1983, the
section of the eastbound carriageway of Des Voeux Road West between Hill Road and a point
approximately 45 metres west of its junction with Whitty Street will be designated as public light bus
restricted zone from 7.00 a.m. to mid-night daily.
Within the restricted zone, drivers of all public light buses except those authorized in writing by
the Authority will be prohibited from picking up/setting down passengers.
The extent of the restricted zone will be indicated by the appropriate traffic signs.
P. F. LEEDS Commissioner for Transport
G.N. 1264 TRANSPORT DEPARTMENT
ROAD TRAFFIC (ROADS AND SIGNS) REGULATIONS (Chapter 220)
In exercise of the powers vested in me under regulation 18A(1)(4) of the Road Traffic (Roads and
Signs) Regulations, I hereby direct that the restricted zone for all vehicles for the period from 8.00
p.m. to 11.00 a.m. daily on the eastern lane of the taxi stand in the Tsim Sha Tsui Ferry Concourse
will be rescinded with effect from 10.00 a.m. on Monday, 25 April 1983.
P. F. LEEDS Commissioner for Transport
