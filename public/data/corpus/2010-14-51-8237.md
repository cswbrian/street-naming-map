## English

G.N. 8237 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of HOI TAI STREET in Eastern District, Hong Kong Island as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 1673 dated 4 November 1960 with immediate effect:— Description Name The street is approximately 345 metres long. It starts from its junction with Hoi Chak Street and Finnie Street. It ends at a cul-de-sac near Quarry Bay Park. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. HKRM29. HOI TAI STREET Plan No. HKRM29 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Hong Kong at 18th Floor, Southorn Centre, 130–150 Hennessy Road, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 24 December 2010 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands

## 中文
