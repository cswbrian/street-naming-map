## English

G.N. 8864 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the descriptions of CHEONG WAN ROAD and ON WAN ROAD in the districts of Kowloon City and Yau Tsim Mong, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 1565 dated 9 March 2001 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 1 170 metres long. It starts from its junction with Gillies Avenue South and Hung Hom South Road. It ends at its junction with Austin Road and Chatham Road South. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM19a. CHEONG WAN ROAD The road is approximately 270 metres long. It starts from its junction with Cheong Wan Road and runs north-easterly ending at another junction with Cheong Wan Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured blue on Plan No. KRM19a. ON WAN ROAD A copy of Plan No. KRM19a and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 13 November 2015 KWOK Chi-wo for Director of Lands

## 中文
