## English

G.N. 1075 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following roads in the North District, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 445 metres long. It starts from its junction with Lin Ma Hang Road, and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. DNRM108. HEUNG LIN ROAD The road is approximately 250 metres long. It starts from its junction with Lin Ma Hang Road, and ends at its junction with an unnamed road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured yellow on Plan No. DNRM108. LIN CHUK ROAD A copy of Plan No. DNRM108 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the District Survey Office, North at 5th Floor, North District Government Offices, 3 Pik Fung Road, Fanling, New Territories; in the Map Publications Centre, Hong Kong at 6th Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 17 February 2023 CHU Siu-ki for Director of Lands
