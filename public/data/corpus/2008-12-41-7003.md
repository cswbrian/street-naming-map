## English

G.N. 7003 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following streets in the district of Yau Tsim Mong, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 235 metres long. It starts from its junction with Queen Elizabeth Hospital Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. KRM62. NIGHTINGALE ROAD The road is approximately 405 metres long. It starts from its junction with Gascoigne Road and ends in a loop on the podium of Queen Elizabeth Hospital. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown in pattern of black circles on Plan No. KRM62. QUEEN ELIZABETH HOSPITAL ROAD The street is approximately 105 metres long. It starts from the beginning of a retaining wall at the east side of Queen Elizabeth Hospital Road and ends at a subway under Queen Elizabeth Hospital Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured grey on Plan No. KRM62 QUEEN ELIZABETH HOSPITAL PATH Plan No. KRM62 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong and in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 10 October 2008 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands

## 中文

第 7003 號公告 地政總署 街道命名 現根據 《公眾衞生及市政條例》 ( 第 132 章 ) 第 111C(1)( a ) 條公布，下列位於九龍油尖旺區的街道， 由本公告日期起，採用以下名稱： 說明 名稱 這道路長約 235 米，以其與伊利沙伯醫院路的交界處為起點，至其 盡頭處終結。位置及路線在第 KRM62 號圖則上以黑網點標明。 南丁格爾路 這道路長約 405 米，以其與加士居道的交界處為起點，至伊利沙伯 醫院平台上的迴路終結。位置及路線在第 KRM62 號圖則上以黑圓 形圖案標明。 伊利沙伯醫院路 這街道長約 105 米，以伊利沙伯醫院路東面一幅護土牆的開端為起 點，至伊利沙伯醫院路底下的一條行人隧道終結。位置及路線在第 KRM62 號圖則上以灰色標明。 伊利沙伯醫院徑 查閱第 KRM62 號圖則，可到九龍上海街 250 號油麻地停車場大廈 10 樓九龍測量處、香港 北角渣華道 333 號北角政府合署 23 樓地政總署測繪處，或香港銅鑼灣高士威道 66 號香港中央 圖書館 5 樓地圖圖書館。 2008 年 10 月 10 日 地政總署署長 ( 邵偉青代行 )
