G.N. 2623 TRANSPORT DEPARTMENT
ROAD TRAFFIC (ROADS AND SIGNS) REGULATIONS (Chapter 220)
PUBLIC LIGHT BUS RESTRICTED ZONE ON LOK SHAN ROAD
In exercise of the powers vested in me under regulation 18A(1)(5) of the Road Traffic (Roads and
| Signs) Regulations, I hereby direct that with effect from 10.00 a.m. on 12 August 1983, the section of
Lok Shan Road between Maidstone Road and Ma Tau Wai Road, will be designated a 24-hour
public light bus restricted zone. Within the restricted zone, all public light bus will be prohibited
from picking up and setting down passengers.
The extent of the restricted zone will be indicated by appropriate traffic signs and carriageway
markings.
P. F. LEEDS Commissioner for Transport
