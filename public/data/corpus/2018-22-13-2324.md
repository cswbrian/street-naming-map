## English

G.N. 2324 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of CHUI YU ROAD in the districts of Sham Shui Po, and Yau Tsim Mong, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 2633 dated 29 May 1998 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 580 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tai Kok Tsui Road and Tung Chau Street and ends at its junction with Sham Mong Road with a branch connecting Tung Chau Street, Boundary Street and Nam Cheong Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM113. CHUI YU ROAD A copy of Plan No. KRM113 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 29 March 2018 LEUNG Kin-wah for Director of Lands
