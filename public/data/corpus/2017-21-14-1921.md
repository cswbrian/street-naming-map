## English

G.N. 1921 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of WEST KOWLOON HIGHWAY in the districts of Sham Shui Po and Yau Tsim Mong, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 2489 dated 22 May 1998; and the description of LAI CHI KOK ROAD in the districts of Sham Shui Po and Yau Tsim Mong, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 2641 dated 13 October 1978 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 4 200 metres long. It starts from its junction with Western Harbour Crossing and ends at its junction with Tsing Kwai Highway, with its slip roads joining Yau Ma Tei Interchange, Lin Cheung Road, Lai Cheung Road and Jordan Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM95. WEST KOWLOON HIGHWAY The road is approximately 3 550 metres long. It starts from its junction with Nathan Road and ends at its two different junctions with Kwai Chung Road and an unnamed road, with a branch road ends near Banyan Garden. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM96. LAI CHI KOK ROAD A copy of Plan No. KRM95 and KRM96 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 7 April 2017 LEUNG Kin-wah for Director of Lands

## 中文

第 1921 號公告 地政總署 街道命名 現謹公布， 1998 年 5 月 22 日第 2489 號政府公告載述於九龍深水埗區及油尖旺區的西九龍公路 的說明；以及 1978 年 10 月 13 日第 2641 號政府公告載述於九龍深水埗區及油尖旺區的荔枝角 道的說明，分別由以下說明取代，即時生效： 說明 名稱 這道路長約 4 200 米，以其與西區海底隧道的交界處為起點，至其 與青葵公路的交界處終結，並以多段支路連接油麻地交匯處、連翔 道、麗翔道及佐敦道。其位置及路線在第 KRM95 號圖則上以粉紅 色標示。 西九龍公路 這道路長約 3 550 米，以其與彌敦道的交界處為起點，至其分別與 葵涌道及一條未命名道路的兩個交界處終結，及其支路鄰近泓景臺 終結。其位置及路線在第 KRM96 號圖則上以粉紅色標示。 荔枝角道 查閱第 KRM95 及 KRM96 號圖則及本公告的副本，可瀏覽地政總署網站 (www.landsd.gov. hk) 政府公告一欄，或到九龍上海街 250 號油麻地停車場大廈 10 樓九龍測量處、香港北角渣華 道 333 號北角政府合署 23 樓地政總署測繪處，或香港銅鑼灣高士威道 66 號香港中央圖書館 5 樓地圖圖書館。 2017 年 4 月 7 日 地政總署署長 ( 梁建華代行 )
