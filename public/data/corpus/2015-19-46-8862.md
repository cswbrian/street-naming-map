## English

G.N. 8862 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in the district of Yuen Long, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 125 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tan Kwai Tsuen Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. YLRM176. TAN KWAI TSUEN LANE A copy of Plan No. YLRM176 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Yuen Long at 8th Floor, Yuen Long Government Offices, 2 Kiu Lok Square, Yuen Long, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 13 November 2015 KWOK Chi-wo for Director of Lands
