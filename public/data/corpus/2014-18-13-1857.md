## English

G.N. 1857 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Kwun Tong, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 2 430 metres long. It starts from its junction with Clear Water Bay Road and ends at its junction with Po Lam Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM73. ON SAU ROAD A copy of Plan No. KRM73 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 28 March 2014 NG Kwok-wai for Director of Lands
