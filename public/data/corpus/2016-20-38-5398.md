## English

G.N. 5398 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in the district of Islands, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 160 metres long. It starts from its junction with an unnamed road near the northeast corner of Tung Wan Villa. It ends at its junction with an unnamed road near the building of house number 15 at Shan Ting Tsuen. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. ISRM95. PENG CHAU HO KING STREET A copy of Plan No. ISRM95 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Islands at 18th Floor, Harbour Building, 38 Pier Road, Central, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 23 September 2016 LEUNG Kin-wah for Director of Lands
