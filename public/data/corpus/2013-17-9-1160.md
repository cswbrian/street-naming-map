## English

G.N. 1160 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of LOK WO SHA LANE in the district of Sha Tin, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 2923 dated 25 August 1989 with immediate effect:— Description Name The street is approximately 210 metres long. It starts from its junction with Nin Wah Road and ends at its junction with an unnamed road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. STRM1161a. LOK WO SHA LANE A copy of Plan No. STRM1161a and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Sha Tin at 12th Floor, Sha Tin Government Offices, 1 Sheung Wo Che Road, Sha Tin, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 1 March 2013 CHENG Wai-pun for Director of Lands
