## English

G.N. 708 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following roads in the district of Yuen Long, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 910 metres long. It starts from its junction with Fung Kat Heung Road, and ends at its junction with Shui Mei Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured yellow on Plan No. YLRM215. MEI FUNG ROAD The road is approximately 1 510 metres long. It starts from its junction with Castle Peak Road—Tam Mi, and ends at its junction with an unnamed road near Pak Wai Tsuen. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. YLRM215. SHUI MEI ROAD A copy of Plan No. YLRM215 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Yuen Long at 8th Floor, Yuen Long Government Offices, 2 Kiu Lok Square, Yuen Long, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 5 February 2021 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
