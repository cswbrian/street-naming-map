## English

G.N. 6569 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of ON LAI STREET in the district of Sha Tin, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 229 dated 10 January 2003 with immediate effect:— Description Name The street is approximately 330 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tai Chung Kiu Road and ends at its junction with On Kwan Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. STRM1159d. ON LAI STREET A copy of Plan No. STRM1159d and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Sha Tin at 12th Floor, Sha Tin Government Offices, 1 Sheung Wo Che Road, Sha Tin, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 1 November 2013 CHENG Wai-pun for Director of Lands
