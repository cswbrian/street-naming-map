## English

G.N. 6493 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following streets in the district of Kowloon City, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 265 metres long. It starts from its junction with Shing Kai Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM74. MUK CHUI STREET The street is approximately 80 metres long. It starts from the roundabout joining Shing Kai Road and Eastern Road. It ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured orange on Plan No. KRM74. MUK CHUN STREET The street is approximately 280 metres long. It starts from its junction with Shing Kai Road and ends at its junction with Muk Chui Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured yellow on Plan No. KRM74. MUK HUNG STREET The street is approximately 445 metres long. It starts from its junction with Shing Kai Road and Kai Shing Street. It ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured green on Plan No. KRM74. MUK ON STREET The road is approximately 1 050 metres long. It starts from the roundabout joining Eastern Road and Muk Chun Street. It ends at about 100 metres passing its junction with Muk On Street and Kai Shing Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured blue on Plan No. KRM74. SHING KAI ROAD The road is approximately 320 metres long. It starts from its junction with Cheung Yip Street and ends at its junction with an unnamed road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured blue on Plan No. KRM75. SHING CHEONG ROAD The road is approximately 1 390 metres long. It starts near the fire boat berth cum public landing steps and ends at a cul-de-sac near the cruise terminal under construction. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM75. SHING FUNG ROAD A copy of Plan Nos. KRM74 and KRM75, and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 28 September 2012 CHENG Wai-pun for Director of Lands
