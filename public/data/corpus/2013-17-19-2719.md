## English

G.N. 2719 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following streets in the district of Yuen Long, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 52 metres long. It is an extension of the existing Ha Mei Road. It starts from its junction with Ping Ha Road and joins the existing Ha Mei Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured green on Plan No. YLRM165. HA MEI ROAD The road is approximately 490 metres long. It starts from its junction with Castle Peak Road—Tam Mi and ends at its junction with Kam Pok Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. YLRM167. KAM POK ROAD EAST The road is approximately 215 metres long. It starts from its junction with Kam Pok Road and Pok Wai South Road. It ends at a cul-de- sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured blue on Plan No. YLRM167. KAM POK ROAD WEST The street is approximately 42 metres long. It starts from its junction with Castle Peak Road—Yuen Long and ends at its junction with Sau Fu Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured blue on Plan No. YLRM168. KIU SAU PATH The street is approximately 49 metres long. It starts from its junction with Sau Fu Street and ends at its junction with Fook Tak Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. YLRM168. KIU TAK PATH The road is approximately 360 metres long. It is an extension of the existing Tin Wah Road. It starts from its junction with Lau Fau Shan Road and ends at its junction with Tin Ying Road where it joins the existing Tin Wah Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured blue on Plan No. YLRM165. TIN WAH ROAD A copy of Plan Nos. YLRM165, YLRM167 and YLRM168, and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Yuen Long at 8th Floor, Yuen Long Government Offices, 2 Kiu Lok Square, Yuen Long, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 10 May 2013 CHENG Wai-pun for Director of Lands

## 中文

第 2719 號公告 地政總署 街道命名 現根據 《公眾衞生及市政條例》 ( 第 132 章 ) 第 111C(1)( a ) 條公布，下述位於新界元朗區的街道， 由本公告日期起，採用以下名稱： 說明 名稱 這道路長約 52 米，是現時廈尾路的延展部分，以其與屏廈路的交界 處為起點，並與現時的廈尾路連接。其位置及路線在第 YLRM165 號圖則上以綠色標明。 廈尾路 這道路長約 490 米，以其與青山公路 ——潭尾段的交界處為起點， 至其與錦壆路的交界處終結。其位置及路線在第 YLRM167 號圖則 上以粉紅色標明。 錦壆路東 這道路長約 215 米，以其與錦壆路及壆圍南路的交界處為起點，至 其盡頭處終結。其位置及路線在第 YLRM167 號圖則上以藍色標明。 錦壆路西 這街道長約 42 米，以其與青山公路 ——元朗段的交界處為起點， 至其與壽富街的交界處終結。其位置及路線在第 YLRM168 號圖則 上以藍色標明。 橋壽徑 這街道長約 49 米，以其與壽富街的交界處為起點，至其與福德街的 交界處終結。其位置及路線在第 YLRM168 號圖則上以粉紅色標明。 橋德徑 這道路長約 360 米，是現時天華路的延展部分，以其與流浮山道的 交界處為起點，至其與天影路的交界處終結，並與現時的天華路連 接。其位置及路線在第 YLRM165 號圖則上以藍色標明。 天華路 查閱第 YLRM165, YLRM167 和 YLRM168 號圖則及本公告的副本，可瀏覽地政總署網站 (www.landsd.gov.hk) 政府公告一欄，或到新界元朗橋樂坊 2 號元朗政府合署 8 樓元朗測量處、 香港北角渣華道 333 號北角政府合署 23 樓地政總署測繪處，或香港銅鑼灣高士威道 66 號香港 中央圖書館 5 樓地圖圖書館。 2013 年 5 月 10 日 地政總署署長 ( 鄭偉斌代行 )
