## English

G.N. 4532 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the descriptions of a section of KWONG SIN STREET and a section of NGAU PEI SHA STREET in the district of Sha Tin, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace those set out in G.N. 416 dated 6 February 1987 and G.N. 8237 dated 15 December 2000 respectively with immediate effect:— Description Name The street is approximately 495 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ngau Pei Sha Street, and ends at its junction with Siu Lek Yuen Road where it joins the other section of Kwong Sin Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured yellow on Plan No. STRM1160b. KWONG SIN STREET The street is approximately 500 metres long. It starts from its junction with Sha Tin Wai Road where it joins the other section of Ngau Pei Sha Street, and ends at its junction with Kwong Sin Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. STRM1160b. NGAU PEI SHA STREET A copy of Plan No. STRM1160b and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Sha Tin at 12th Floor, Sha Tin Government Offices, 1 Sheung Wo Che Road, Sha Tin, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 2 August 2013 CHENG Wai-pun for Director of Lands

## 中文

第 4532 號公告 地政總署 街道命名 現謹公布， 1987 年 2 月 6 日第 416 號政府公告及 2000 年 12 月 15 日第 8237 號政府公告分別載 述新界沙田區一段廣善街及一段牛皮沙街的說明，由以下說明取代，即時生效： 說明 名稱 這街道長約 495 米，以其與牛皮沙街的交界處為起點，至其與小瀝源路及另 一段廣善街的交界處終結。位置及路線在第 STRM1160b 號圖則上以黄色標 示。 廣善街 這街道長約 500 米，以其與沙田圍路及另一段牛皮沙街的交界處為起點，至 其與廣善街的交界處終結。位置及路線在第 STRM1160b 號圖則上以粉紅色 標示。 牛皮沙街 查閱第 STRM1160b 號圖則及本公告的副本，可瀏覽地政總署網站 (www.landsd.gov.hk) 政 府公告一欄，或到新界沙田上禾輋路 1 號沙田政府合署 12 樓沙田測量處、香港北角渣華道 333 號北角政府合署 23 樓地政總署測繪處，或香港銅鑼灣高士威道 66 號香港中央圖書館 5 樓 地圖圖書館。 2013 年 8 月 2 日 地政總署署長 ( 鄭偉斌代行 )
