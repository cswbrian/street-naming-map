## English

G.N. 3610 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of KWAI YAN ROAD in the district of Kwai Tsing, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 4036 dated 24 December 1982 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 260 metres long. It starts from its junction with Kwai Foo Road and ends at its junction with Kwai Yi Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. KTRM75a. KWAI YAN ROAD Plan No. KTRM75a may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Tsuen Wan and Kwai Tsing at 15th Floor, Tsuen Wan Government Offices, 38 Sai Lau Kok Road, Tsuen Wan, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 18 June 2010 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
