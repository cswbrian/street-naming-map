## English

G.N. 4524 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of NGA CHEUNG ROAD in the district of Yau Tsim Mong, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 2488 dated 22 May 1998 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 1 210 metres long. It starts from its junction with Lin Cheung Road and ends at its two junctions with Austin Road West, with its branches connecting Jordan Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM101. NGA CHEUNG ROAD A copy of Plan No. KRM101 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 30 June 2017 LEUNG Kin-wah for Director of Lands
