## English

G.N. 5324 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of the following road in the Islands District, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace those set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. The road is approximately 700 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ying Hei Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured orange on Plan No. ISRM108. YING TUNG ROAD G.N. 3150 dated 30 May 2014 G.N. 6438 dated 11 November 2016 A copy of Plan No. ISRM108 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the Map Publications Centre, Hong Kong at 6th Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 6 September 2024 CHU Siu-ki for Director of Lands
