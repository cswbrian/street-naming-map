## English

G.N. 3897 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following roads in the Kowloon City District, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 410 metres long. It starts from its junction with Shing Fung Road, and ends at its junction with Shing Cheong Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured brown on Plan No. KRM133. KAI TAK BRIDGE ROAD The road is approximately 50 metres long. It starts from its junction with Shing Fung Road, and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured green on Plan No. KRM133. SHING FU LANE The road is approximately 60 metres long. It starts from its junction with Shing Fung Road, and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured blue on Plan No. KRM133. SHING YU LANE The road is approximately 235 metres long. It starts from a cul-de-sac in the north, and runs southwesterly for about 60 metres where it meets Shing Fung Road. It continues to run for about 175 metres and ends at another cul-de-sac in the south. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured purple on Plan No. KRM133. SHING KING STREET A copy of Plan No. KRM133 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 3rd Floor, South Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 25 June 2021 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
