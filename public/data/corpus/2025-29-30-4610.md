## English

G.N. 4610 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of the following road in the Islands District, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace those set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. The road is approximately 2 020 metres long. It starts from its junction with Yi Tung Road, and ends at its junction with Hiu Tung Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured light blue on Plan No. ISRM110a. YING HEI ROAD G.N. 8173 dated 24 December 1999 A copy of Plan No. ISRM110a and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the Map Publications Centre, Hong Kong at 6th Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 25 July 2025 CHU Siu-ki for Director of Lands
