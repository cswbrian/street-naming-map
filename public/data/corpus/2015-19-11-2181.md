## English

G.N. 2181 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in the district of Tai Po, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 530 metres long. It starts from its junction with Lam Kam Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. TPRM119. HANG KAI LANE A copy of Plan No. TPRM119 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Tai Po at 5th Floor, Tai Po Government Offices, 1 Ting Kok Road, Tai Po, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 13 March 2015 NG Kwok-wai for Director of Lands
