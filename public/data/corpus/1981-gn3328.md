ROAD TRAFFIC (TEMPORARY CAR PARKS) REGULATIONS (Chapter 220)

In exercise of the powers vested in me under regulation 3(1) of the Road Traffic
(Temporary Car Parks) Regulations, Chapter 220, I hereby give notification that with
effect from 7.00 a.m. on 7 November 1981 the western half of the Ho Man Tin Lorry
Park, which is bounded on the north and east by Good Shepherd Street and on the
south-west by Chung Hau Street, will be temporarily closed for surfacing work for
about eight weeks.
5 November 198] A. T. ARMSTRONG-WRIGHT Commissioner for Transport
