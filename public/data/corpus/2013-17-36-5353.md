## English

G.N. 5353 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of BERWICK STREET in the district of Sham Shui Po, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 804 dated 24 June 1950 with immediate effect:— Description Name The street is approximately 480 metres long. It starts from its junction with Shek Kip Mei Street and ends at Saviour Lutheran Church. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. KRM76. BERWICK STREET A copy of Plan No. KRM76 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 6 September 2013 CHENG Wai-pun for Director of Lands
