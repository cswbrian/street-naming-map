## English

G.N. 3863 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the districts of Kwun Tong and Wong Tai Sin, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The interchange connects Clear Water Bay Road, Lung Cheung Road, Kwun Tong Road and Prince Edward Road East. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM86. CHOI HUNG INTERCHANGE A copy of Plan No. KRM86 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 8 July 2016 LEUNG Kin-wah for Director of Lands
