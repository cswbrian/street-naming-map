## English

G.N. 7461 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Yuen Long, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 1 920 metres long. It starts from its junction with Fung Kong Tsuen Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured red on Plan No. YLRM160a. KAI PAK LING ROAD Plan No. YLRM160a may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Yuen Long at 8th Floor, Yuen Long Government Offices, 2 Kiu Lok Square, Yuen Long, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 11 November 2011 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
