## English

G.N. 7381 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following roads in the Yau Tsim Mong District, Kowloon City District and Kwun Tong District, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 8 750 metres long. It starts from its junctions with Tseung Lam Highway and Lam Tin Interchange respectively, and ends at its junctions with Lin Cheung Road, Yau Ma Tei Interchange, West Kowloon Highway and Lai Cheung Road respectively, with branches connecting Shing Cheong Road and Kai Tak Interchange. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM147. CENTRAL KOWLOON BYPASS The interchange connecting Shing Kai Road, Kai Cheung Road, Kai Fuk Road and Central Kowloon Bypass. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown hatched green on Plan No. KRM147. KAI TAK INTERCHANGE A copy of Plan No. KRM147 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the Map Publications Centre, Hong Kong at 6th Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 21 November 2025 CHU Siu-ki for Director of Lands
