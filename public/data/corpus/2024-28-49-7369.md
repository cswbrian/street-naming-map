## English

G.N. 7369 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the Kowloon City District, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 42 metres long. It starts from its junction with Shing Cheong Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured blue on Plan No. KRM144. SHING YAN LANE A copy of Plan No. KRM144 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the Map Publications Centre, Hong Kong at 6th Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 6 December 2024 CHU Siu-ki for Director of Lands
