## English

G.N. 6773 Lands Department STREET NAMES Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following streets in the district of Islands, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 165 metres long. It starts from its junction with Cheung Chau Peak Road and ends at its junction with Tai San Back Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. ISRM84. SCHOOL ROAD The street is approximately 325 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tai San Street. It ends at its junction with Hing Lung Back Street, with a branch ends between two buildings of house number 137 and 207 of the street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured yellow on Plan No. ISRM84. TAI SAN BACK STREET The road is approximately 310 metres long. It starts from its junction with Chung Hing Back Street and ends at its junction with School Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured green on Plan No. ISRM84. TAI TSOI YUEN ROAD Plan No. ISRM84 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Islands at 18th Floor, Harbour Building, 38 Pier Road, Central, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong and in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 14 October 2011 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
