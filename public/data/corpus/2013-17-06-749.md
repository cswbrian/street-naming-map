G.N. 749 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Tai Po, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 570 metres long. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. TPRM112.
