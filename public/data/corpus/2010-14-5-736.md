## English

G.N. 736 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of YU ON STREET in Tai Po District, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 2124 dated 3 July 1987 with immediate effect:— Description Name The street is approximately 445 metres long. It starts from its southern junction with Sam Mun Tsai Road. It ends at its northern junction with Sam Mun Tsai Road and at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. TPRM105. YU ON STREET Plan No. TPRM105 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Tai Po at 5th Floor, Tai Po Government Offices, 1 Ting Kok Road, Tai Po, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 5 February 2010 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
