## English

G.N. 5316 LANDS DEPARTMENT STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Tuen Mun, New Territories will be known as from the date of this notice as described hereunder:— Description Name LUNG MUN ROAD Plan No. TMRM 89a may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Tuen Mun at 5th Floor, Tuen Mun Government Offices, 1 Tuen Hi Road, Tuen Mun, New Territories; and in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong. 11 August 2000 C. T. LAU for Director of Lands The road is approximately 1 550 metres long. It is an extension to the existing Lung Mun Road. It starts at its junction with the existing Lung Mun Road near Pillar Point and ends at another junction with Lung Mun Road and Lung Yiu Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. TMRM 89a.
