## English

G.N. 1970 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in the district of Wan Chai, Hong Kong Island as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 140 metres long. It starts from its junction with Lung Wui Road, it ends at its junction with Lung Wo Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured yellow on Plan No. HKRM48. LUNG HOP STREET A copy of Plan No. H KRM 48 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Hong Kong at 18th Floor, Southorn Centre , 130–150 Hennessy Road, Wan Chai, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 15 March 2019 CHAN Siu-bun for Director of Lands
