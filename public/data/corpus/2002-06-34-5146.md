## English

G.N. 5146 LANDS DEPARTMENT STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Yuen Long, New Territories as described hereunder will be known as from the date of this notice:— Description Name Plan No. YLRM105a may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Yuen Long at 8th Floor, Yuen Long Government Offices and Tai Kiu Market, 2 Kiu Lok Square, Yuen Long, New Territories; and in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong. 23 August 2002 P. K. AU YEUNG for Director of Lands POK WAI SOUTH ROAD The road is approximately 1 940 metres long. It starts from its junction with Castle Peak Road — Tam Mi and ends at its junction with Kam Pok Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown hatched black on Plan No. YLRM105a.
