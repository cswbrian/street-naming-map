## English

G.N. 2547 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the Southern District, Hong Kong Island as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 490 metres long. It starts from its junction with Shum Wan Road, and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured blue on Plan No. HKRM55. OCEAN DRIVE A copy of Plan No. HKRM55 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Hong Kong at 18th Floor, Southorn Centre, 130–150 Hennessy Road, Wan Chai, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 30 April 2021 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
