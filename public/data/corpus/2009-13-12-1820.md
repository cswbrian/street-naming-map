## English

G.N. 1820 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in Tuen Mun District, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 405 metres long. It is an extension of the existing Yip Wong Road and ends at a cul-de-sac near San Shek Wan Substation. Its location and alignment are more particulary as shown coloured pink on Plan No. TMRM110. YIP WONG ROAD Plan No. TMRM110 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Tuen Mun at 5th Floor, Tuen Mun Government Offices, 1 Tuen Hi Road, Tuen Mun, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong and in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 20 March 2009 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
