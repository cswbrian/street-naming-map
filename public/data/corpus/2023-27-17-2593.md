## English

G.N. 2593 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the Kowloon City District, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 275 metres long. It starts from a cul-de-sac near the MTR Sung Wong Toi Station, and ends at another cul-de-sac in the northeast. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM140. MUK WO STREET A copy of Plan No. KRM140 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 3rd Floor, South Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon; in the Map Publications Centre, Hong Kong at 6th Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 28 April 2023 CHU Siu-ki for Director of Lands
