## English

G.N. 6215 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the North District, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 700 metres long. It starts from its junction with Jockey Club Road and ends at its junction with an unnamed road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. DNRM113. YUK TONG PATH A copy of Plan No. DNRM113 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the District Survey Office, North at 5th Floor, North District Government Offices, 3 Pik Fung Road, Fanling, New Territories; in the Map Publications Centre, Hong Kong at 6th Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 18 October 2024 CHU Siu-ki for Director of Lands
