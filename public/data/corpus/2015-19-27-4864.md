## English

G.N. 4864 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of KUNG UM ROAD which is set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 2215 dated 28 June 1991; the description of MA TIN ROAD which is set out hereunder will replace those set out in G.N. 2481 dated 29 September 1978 and G.N. 1738 dated 15 June 1984; and the description of MA TONG ROAD which is set out hereunder will replace those set out in G.N. 2215 dated 28 June 1991 and G.N. 2493 dated 27 May 2005 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 3 150 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ma Tin Road and Ma Tong Road. It ends at a cul-de- sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured green on Plan No. YLRM174. KUNG UM ROAD The road is approximately 810 metres long. It starts from its junction with Kau Yuk Road and ends at its junction with Town Park Road North and Town Park Road South. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. YLRM174. MA TIN ROAD The road is approximately 670 metres long. It starts from its junction with Kung Um Road and Ma Tin Road. It ends at its junction with Fung Cheung Road and Fung Ki Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured yellow on Plan No. YLRM174. MA TONG ROAD A copy of Plan No. YLRM174 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Yuen Long at 8th Floor, Yuen Long Government Offices, 2 Kiu Lok Square, Yuen Long, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 3 July 2015 NG Kwok-wai for Director of Lands

## 中文
