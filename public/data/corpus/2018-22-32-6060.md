## English

G.N. 6060 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of following streets in the districts of Sham Shui Po, Wong Tai Sin, Kowloon City and Kwun Tong, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. TAI HOM ROAD G.N. 4197 dated 22 November 1991 The road is approximately 420 metres long. It starts from its junction with Wong Tai Sin Road and ends at its junction with Chuk Yuen Road. MA CHAI HANG ROAD G.N. 288 dated 6 February 1976 G.N. 327 Dated 16 January 1986 The street is approximately 850 metres long. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown stippled black on Plan No. KRM111.
