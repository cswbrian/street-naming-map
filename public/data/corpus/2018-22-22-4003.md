## English

G.N. 4003 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of TAI PO ROAD in the district of Sham Shui Po, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 250 dated 29 April 1927 and G.N. 262 dated 2 May 1930 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 3 050 metres long. It starts from its two junctions with Cheung Sha Wan Road, and ends at its junction with Caldecott Road and Tai Po Road—Piper’s Hill, with branches connecting Castle Peak Road, Kweilin Street and Ching Cheung Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM110. TAI PO ROAD A copy of Plan No. KRM110 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 1 June 2018 LEUNG Kin-wah for Director of Lands
