## English

G.N. 1088 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of North, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 340 metres long. It starts from its junction with Lung Ma Road at the west and ends at another junction with Lung Ma Road at the east. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. DNRM109. LUNG CHUN ROAD A copy of Plan No. DNRM109 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, North at 5th Floor, North District Government Offices, 3 Pik Fung Road, Fanling, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 26 February 2021 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
