## English

G.N. 5248 LANDS DEPARTMENT DECLARATION TO CHANGE STREET NAME With reference to G.N. 3020 dated 2 May 2003, it is hereby declared under Section 111C(1)( b ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that Leung Tin Lane is renamed as On Tin Street in Kwun Tong District, Kowloon, as described hereunder from the date of this notice:— Description Name Plan No. KRM31 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon and in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong. 1 August 2003 P. K. AU YEUNG for Director of Lands ON TIN STREET The street is approximately 140 metres long. It is an extension of existing On Tin Street and ends at its junction with Tak Tin Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. KRM31.
