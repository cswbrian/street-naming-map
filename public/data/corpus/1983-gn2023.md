OUTLINE ZONING PLAN NO. LTW/146L
Pursuant to section 7 of the Town Planning Ordinance, a draft Amendment Plan for the above area
incorporating the amendments described on the following schedule can be seen until 21 July 1983
during normal office hours by any person at: the District Office (Central and Western) Public
Enquiry Sub-office, Central Government Offices, West Wing Entrance, Hong Kong; the Town
Planning Division, 4th floor, Murray Building, Garden Road, Hong Kong and the District Office
. (Tsuen Wan), 2nd floor, Fou Wah Centre, 210 Castle Peak Road, Tsuen Wan.

In accordance with section 7(4) of the Ordinance, any person affected by the scheduled
amendments may up until the above-mentioned date, inform the Town Planning Board of his
objection by writing to the Secretary, Town Planning Board, c/o Lands Department, Murray
Building, Garden Road, Hong Kong, setting out the reasons for his objection and any alteration
which would remove his objection.

Copies of the draft Amendment Plan No. LTW/146M are available at the Survey Division, Lands
Department, 5th floor, Murray Building, Garden Road, Hong Kong at $10 each uncoloured and
$60 each coloured.
