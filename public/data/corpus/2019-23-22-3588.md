## English

G.N. 3588 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in the district of Yau Tsim Mong, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 560 metres long. It starts from its junction with an unnamed road, and ends at its junction with Austin Road West. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM122. MUSEUM DRIVE A copy of Plan No. KRM122 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 3rd Floor, South Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 31 May 2019 CHAN Siu-bun for Director of Lands
