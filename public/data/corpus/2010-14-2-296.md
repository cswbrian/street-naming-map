## English

G.N. 296 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of LUNG WUI ROAD in Central and Western District, Hong Kong Island as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 1882 dated 17 October 1997 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 190 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tim Mei Avenue and ends at its junction with Fenwick Pier Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. HKRM30. LUNG WUI ROAD Plan No. HKRM30 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Hong Kong at 18th Floor, Southorn Centre, 130–150 Hennessy Road, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 15 January 2010 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
