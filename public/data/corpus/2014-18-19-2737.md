## English

G.N. 2737 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following roads in Tuen Mun District, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 300 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tsing Yick Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured blue on Plan No. TMRM123a. MAK YUEN WAI ROAD The road is approximately 610 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ng Lau Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. TMRM123a. TSING YICK ROAD A copy of Plan No. TMRM123a and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Tuen Mun at 5th Floor, Tuen Mun Government Offices, 1 Tuen Hi Road, Tuen Mun, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 9 May 2014 NG Kwok-wai for Director of Lands
