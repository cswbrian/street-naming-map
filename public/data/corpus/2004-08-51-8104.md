## English

G.N. 8104 LANDS DEPARTMENT STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following streets in the district of Islands, New Territories as described hereunder will be known as from the date of this notice:— Description Name PARK PROMENADE The street is approximately 910 metres long. It starts from the proposed MTR Station and ends at the cul-de-sac near the proposed pier. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured yellow with hatched black on Plan No. ISRM61. SEA POINT ROAD The street is approximately 490 metres long. It starts from its junction with Magic Road and ends at the cul-de-sac near Sze Pak Tsui. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured orange on Plan No. ISRM61. LONG YAN ROAD The street is approximately 290 metres long. It starts from its junction with Magic Road and ends at the cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured yellow on Plan No. ISRM61. CHAK YAN ROAD The street is approximately 170 metres long. It starts from its junction with Fantasy Road and ends at its junction with Long Yan Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured purple on Plan No. ISRM61. WING YAN ROAD The street is approximately 1 250 metres long. It starts from its junction with Fantasy Road. It ends at the entrance of the Power Station and at the cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured brown on Plan No. ISRM61. FANTASY ROAD The street is approximately 1 070 metres long. It starts from the roundabout with Magic Road and Inspiration Drive and ends at the cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured orange with hatched black on Plan No. ISRM61. INSPIRATION DRIVE The street is approximately 420 metres long. It starts from the roundabout with Magic Road and Fantasy Road. It ends at the cul-de-sac at the south and another cul-de-sac near the proposed Water Recreation Centre. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. ISRM61. MAGIC ROAD The street is approximately 2 340 metres long. It starts from the roundabout with Penny’s Bay Highway and Sunny Bay Road and ends at the cul-de-sac near Park Promenade. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured red on Plan No. ISRM61. SUNNY BAY ROAD The street is approximately 2 500 metres long. It starts from the roundabout with Magic Road and Penny’s Bay Highway and ends at the cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured green on Plan No. ISRM61. PENNY’S BAY HIGHWAY The street is approximately 1 090 metres long. It starts from the roundabout with Magic Road and Sunny Bay Road. It ends at the access roads connecting with North Lantau Highway. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured blue on Plan No. ISRM61. Plan No. ISRM61 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Islands at 18th Floor, Harbour Building, 38 Pier Road, Central, Hong Kong; in the District Survey Office, Tsuen Wan and Kwai Tsing at 15th Floor, Tsuen Wan Government Offices, 38 Sai Lau Kok Road, Tsuen Wan, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong and in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 17 December 2004 MAN Wing-kan for Director of Lands
