## English

G.N. 3377 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of the following road in the Sai Kung District, New Territories and the Kwun Tong District, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace those set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. The road consists of two sections and is approximately 580 metres and 280 metres long respectively. One of the sections starts from its junction with On Sau Road, and ends at its cul-de-sac . The other section starts from its another cul-de-sac in the southeast, and ends at its junction with Po Lam Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. SKRM165. ANDERSON ROAD G.N. 2757 dated 10 December 1976 G.N. 6649 dated 22 October 2021 A copy of Plan No. SKRM165 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the District Survey Office, Sai Kung at 3rd Floor, Sai Kung Government Offices, 34 Chan Man Street, Sai Kung, New Territories; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 3rd Floor, South Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon; in the Map Publications Centre, Hong Kong at 6th Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 2 June 2023 CHU Siu-ki for Director of Lands
