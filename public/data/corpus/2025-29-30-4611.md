## English

G.N. 4611 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the Islands District, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 840 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ying Hei Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured purple on Plan No. ISRM110a. KWAI TUNG ROAD The road is approximately 1 520 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ying Hei Road and runs northwest for about 390 metres where it meets Hiu Tung Road. It continues to run northeast for about 740 metres where it meets Kwai Tung Road. Then, it continues to run for about 390 metres and ends at a cul-de- sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. ISRM110a. LONG TUNG ROAD The road is approximately 1 600 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ying Hei Road and runs northwest for about 810 metres where it meets Kwai Tung Road. It continues to run for about 790 metres and ends at its junction with Kam Tung Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured orange on Plan No. ISRM110a. HIU TUNG ROAD The road is approximately 460 metres long. It starts from its junction with Wui Tung Street and runs southeast for about 330 metres where it meets Ying Tung road. It continues to run for about 130 metres and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured dark green on Plan No. ISRM110a. KAM TUNG STREET The road is approximately 440 metres long. It starts from its junction with Kwai Tung Road and ends at its junction with Hiu Tung Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured light green on Plan No. ISRM110a. SIU TUNG STREET The road is approximately 300 metres long. It starts from its junction with Hiu Tung Road and ends at its junction with Siu Tung Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured brown on Plan No. ISRM110a. HAU TUNG STREET The road is approximately 110 metres long. It starts from its junction with Kwai Tung Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured dark blue on Plan No. ISRM110a. CHOI TUNG LANE The road is approximately 110 metres long. It starts from its junction with Long Tung Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured yellow on Plan No. ISRM110a. SHIN TUNG LANE The road is approximately 80 metres long. It starts from its junction with Long Tung Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured red on Plan No. ISRM110a. YAN TUNG LANE A copy of Plan No. ISRM110a and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the Map Publications Centre, Hong Kong at 6th Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 25 July 2025 CHU Siu-ki for Director of Lands
