## English

G.N. 7562 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following roads in the North District, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 205 metres long. It starts from its junction with Castle Peak Road—Chau Tau, and ends at its junction with Heung Tsz Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured yellow on Plan No. DNRM110. PAK SAU ROAD The road is approximately 590 metres long. It starts from its junction with a proposed road near Pak Shek Au, and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. DNRM110. HEUNG TSZ ROAD A copy of Plan No. DNRM110 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the District Survey Office, North at 5th Floor, North District Government Offices, 3 Pik Fung Road, Fanling, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 30 December 2022 CHU Siu-ki for Director of Lands
