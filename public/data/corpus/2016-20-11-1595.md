## English

G.N. 1595 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of YIU SING STREET in Central and Western District, Hong Kong Island as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 6891 dated 19 October 2012 with immediate effect:— Description Name The street is approximately 280 metres long. It starts from its junction with Man Yiu Street and ends at its junction with Lung Wo Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. HKRM43. YIU SING STREET A copy of Plan No. HKRM43 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Hong Kong at 18th Floor, Southorn Centre, 130–150 Hennessy Road, Wan Chai, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 18 March 2016 LEUNG Kin-wah for Director of Lands
