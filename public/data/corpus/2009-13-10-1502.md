## English

G.N. 1502 Lands Department STREET NAMES Notice is hereby given that the descriptions of TING KOK ROAD, FUNG YUEN ROAD, TUNG TSZ ROAD and SHAN LIU ROAD in the district of Tai Po, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace those set out in G.N. 713 dated 3 April 1970 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 9 700 metres long. It starts from its junction with Kwong Fuk Road and Po Nga Road. It ends at its junction with Bride’s Pool Road and Mei Wu Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured red on Plan No. TPRM94a. TING KOK ROAD The road is approximately 890 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ting Kok Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured red on Plan No. TPRM96. FUNG YUEN ROAD The road is approximately 1 360 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ting Kok Road and ends at the gate of Tung Tsz Scout Centre. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured red on Plan No. TPRM97. TUNG TSZ ROAD The road is approximately 730 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ting Kok Road and ends at a fence. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured red on Plan No. TPRM98. SHAN LIU ROAD Plan Nos. TPRM94a, TPRM96, TPRM97 and TPRM98 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Tai Po at 5th Floor, Tai Po Government Offices, 1 Ting Kok Road, Tai Po, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong and in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 6 March 2009 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands

## 中文

第 1502 號公告 地政總署 街道命名 現謹公布， 1970 年 4 月 3 日第 713 號政府公告載述有關新界大埔區汀角路、鳳園路、洞梓路及 山寮路的說明，由以下說明取代，即時生效： 說明 名稱 這道路長約 9 700 米，以其與廣福道及寶雅路的交界處為起點， 至 其 與 新 娘 潭 路 及 美 湖 路 的 交 界 處 終 結。位 置 及 路 線 在 第 TPRM94a 號圖則上以紅色標明。 汀角路 這道路長約 890 米，以其與汀角路的交界處為起點，至其盡頭處 終結。位置及路線在第 TPRM96 號圖則上以紅色標明。 鳳園路 這道路長約 1 360 米，以其與汀角路的交界處為起點，至洞梓童 軍中心的閘門處終結。位置及路線在第 TPRM97 號圖則上以紅 色標明。 洞梓路 這道路長約 730 米，以其與汀角路的交界處為起點，至柵欄處終 結。位置及路線在第 TPRM98 號圖則上以紅色標明。 山寮路 查閱第 TPRM94a 、 TPRM96 、 TPRM97 及 TPRM98 號圖則，可到新界大埔汀角路 1 號大 埔政府合署 5 樓大埔測量處、香港北角渣華道 333 號北角政府合署 23 樓地政總署測繪處，或 香港銅鑼灣高士威道 66 號香港中央圖書館 5 樓地圖圖書館。 2009 年 3 月 6 日 地政總署署長 ( 邵偉青代行 )
