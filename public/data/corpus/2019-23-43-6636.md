## English

G.N. 6636 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Islands, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 185 metres long. It starts from its junction with South Lantau Road, and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. ISRM100. HOI SHA PATH A copy of Plan No. ISRM100 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Islands at 18th Floor, Harbour Building, 38 Pier Road, Central, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 25 October 2019 CHAN Siu-bun for Director of Lands
