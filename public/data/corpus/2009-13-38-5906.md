## English

G.N. 5906 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in Yuen Long District, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 740 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tan Kwai Tsuen Road and ends at its junctions with two unnamed roads. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. YLRM154b. SHUI FU ROAD Plan No. YLRM154b may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Yuen Long at 8th Floor, Yuen Long Government Offices, 2 Kiu Lok Square, Yuen Long, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 18 September 2009 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
