## English

G.N. 8793 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of AUSTIN ROAD WEST in the district of Yau Tsim Mong, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 5530 dated 13 November 1998 and G.N. 1737 dated 12 March 2004 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 910 metres long. It starts from its two junctions with Nga Cheung Road and ends at its junction with Canton Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM115. AUSTIN ROAD WEST A copy of Plan No. KRM115 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 23 November 2018 LEUNG Kin-wah for Director of Lands
