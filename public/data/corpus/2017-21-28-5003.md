## English

G.N. 5003 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of CHI KIANG STREET in the district of Kowloon City, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 334 dated 26 April 1935 and G.N. 1392 dated 28 May 1965 with immediate effect:— Description Name The street is approximately 790 metres long. It starts from its junction with Bailey Street and ends at its junction with Kau Pui Lung Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM102. CHI KIANG STREET A copy of Plan No. KRM102 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 14 July 2017 LEUNG Kin-wah for Director of Lands
