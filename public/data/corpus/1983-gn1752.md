Street section between Hip Wo Street and a point about 100 metres to the east of its junction with ‘

hours and 1900 hours daily.

ROAD TRAFFIC (ROADS AND SIGNS) REGULATIONS (Chapter 220)
In exercise of the powers vested in me under regulation 18A(1)(4) of the Road Traffic (Roads and
Signs) Regulations, I hereby direct that with effect from 10.00 a.m. on Saturday, 4 June 1983, the
section of Road D2N between Ho Pong Street and the unnamed road north of Tuen Mun Clinic will
| be designated as 7.00 a.m. to 9.00 p.m. urban clearway restricted zone.

(b) loading or unloading of goods.

carriageway markings.
