## English

G.N. 7996 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in Tsuen Wan District, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 150 metres long. It starts from its junction with Castle Peak Road—Tsing Lung Tau and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. TWRM176b. HOI LONG PATH Plan No. TWRM176b may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Tsuen Wan and Kwai Tsing at 15th Floor, Tsuen Wan Government Offices, 38 Sai Lau Kok Road, Tsuen Wan, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 18 December 2009 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
