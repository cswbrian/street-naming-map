## English

G.N. 3262 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following roads in the Sai Kung District, New Territories and the Kwun Tong District, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 1 550 metres long. It starts from its junction with Po Lam Road and ends at its second junction with On Hei Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. SKRM161. ON YU ROAD The road is approximately 100 metres long. It starts from its junction with On Yu Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured blue on Plan No. SKRM161. ON YU PATH The road is approximately 810 metres long. It starts from its junction with On Sau Road and ends at its junction with On Yu Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured green on Plan No. SKRM161. ON KIN ROAD The road is approximately 560 metres long. It starts from its junction with On Yu Road at the south, and ends at another junction with On Yu Road at the north. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured orange on Plan No. SKRM161. ON HEI STREET A copy of Plan No. SKRM161 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Sai Kung at 3rd Floor, Sai Kung Government Offices, 34 Chan Man Street, Sai Kung, New Territories; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 3rd Floor, South Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 28 May 2021 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
