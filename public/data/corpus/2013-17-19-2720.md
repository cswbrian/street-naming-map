## English

G.N. 2720 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of PING HA ROAD in the district of Yuen Long, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 4470 dated 13 December 1991 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 3 750 metres long. It starts from its junction with Castle Peak Road—Ping Shan and ends at its junction with Fung Kong Tsuen Road where it joins Lau Fau Shan Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured red on Plan No. YLRM165. PING HA ROAD A copy of Plan No. YLRM165 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Yuen Long at 8th Floor, Yuen Long Government Offices, 2 Kiu Lok Square, Yuen Long, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 10 May 2013 CHENG Wai-pun for Director of Lands
