## English

G.N. 2870 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in the district of Tuen Mun, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 58 metres long. It starts at its junction with Ng Lau Road, and ends at its junction with an unnamed road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. TMRM125. TUNG LEI PATH A copy of Plan No. TMRM125 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Tuen Mun at 5th Floor, Tuen Mun Government Offices, 1 Tuen Hi Road, Tuen Mun, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 26 April 2019 CHAN Siu-bun for Director of Lands
