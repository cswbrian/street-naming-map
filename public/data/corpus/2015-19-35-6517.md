## English

G.N. 6517 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of FUK MAN ROAD in the district of Sai Kung, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 4229 dated 27 December 1985 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 400 metres long. It starts from its junction with Po Tung Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. SKRM156. FUK MAN ROAD A copy of Plan No. SKRM156 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Sai Kung at 3rd Floor, Sai Kung Government Offices, 34 Chan Man Street, Sai Kung, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 28 August 2015 NG Kwok-wai for Director of Lands
