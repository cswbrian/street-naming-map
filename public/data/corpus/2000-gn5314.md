## English

G.N. 5314 LANDS DEPARTMENT STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in the district of Sha Tin, New Territories will be known as from the date of this notice as described hereunder:— Description Name YAT TAI STREET Plan No. STRM 1128 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Sha Tin at 2nd Floor, No. 2, Tung Lo Wan Hill Road, Sha Tin, New Territories; and in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong. 11 August 2000 C. T. LAU for Director of Lands The street is approximately 260 metres long. It starts at its junction with Shui Chuen Au Street and ends at its junction with Sha Kok Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. STRM 1128.
