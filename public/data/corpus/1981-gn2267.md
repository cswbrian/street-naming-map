ROAD TRAFFIC (ROADS AND SIGNS) REGULATIONS (Chapter 220)
PUBLIC LIGHT BUS. RESTRICTION—NGAN MOK STREET
In exercise of the powers vested in me under regulation 18A(1)(b) of the Road Traffic
(Roads and Signs) Regulations, Chapter 220, I hereby direct that with effect from
10.00 a.m. on Saturday, 1 August 1981, a Public Light Bus clearway restriction will
be imposed on the whole of Ngan Mok Street with the exception of the Public Light
Bus Stand there. Within the aforesaid restricted zone, drivers of all public light buses
except those authorized in writing by the Commissioner for Transport are prohibited
ae picking up or setting down passengers between 7.00 a.m. and 12.00 midnight
aily. -
The extent of the restricted zone will be indicated by traffic signs.
