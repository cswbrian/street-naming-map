G.N. 5315 LANDS DEPARTMENT STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in Wong Tai Sin District, Kowloon, will be known as from the date of this notice as described hereunder:— Description Name Plan No. KRM 3a may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon and in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong. 11 August 2000 C. T. LAU for Director of Lands SHUNG WAH STREET The street is approximately 75 metres long. It is an extension to the existing Shung Wah Street and ends at its junction with Po Kong Village Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. KRM 3a.
