## English

G.N. 8315 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in the district of Islands, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 93 metres long. It starts from its junction with Fung Fan Path and ends at its junction with a footpath. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. ISRM82. CHEUNG CHAU ELECTRIC PATH Plan No. ISRM 82 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Islands at 18th Floor, Harbour Building, 38 Pier Road, Central, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong and in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 5 December 2008 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
