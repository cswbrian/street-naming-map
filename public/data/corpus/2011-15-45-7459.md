## English

G.N. 7459 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Tai Po, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 1 680 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tat Wan Road at Sheung Wun Yiu and ends near a pavilion at Ta Tit Yan. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured red on Plan No. TPRM108. WUN YIU ROAD Plan No. TPRM108 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Tai Po at 5th Floor, Tai Po Government Offices, 1 Ting Kok Road, Tai Po, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 11 November 2011 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
