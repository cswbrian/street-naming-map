## English

G.N. 8574 LANDS DEPARTMENT STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Sai Kung, New Territories as described hereunder will be known as from the date of this notice:— Description Name Plan No. SKRM132 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Sai Kung at 3rd Floor, Sai Kung Government Offices, Chan Man Street, Sai Kung, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Governemnt Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong and in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 28 November 2003 P. K. AU YEUNG for Director of Lands PAK FA LAM ROAD The road is approximately 1 170 metres long. It starts from its junction with Fei Ngo Shan Road and an unnamed road. It ends at the cul-de-sac. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. SKRM132.
