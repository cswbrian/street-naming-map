## English

G.N. 9966 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Yuen Long, New Territories as described hereunder will be known with effect from 4 January 2016 as:— Description Name The road is approximately 500 metres long. It extends from the existing Lok Ma Chau Road. It ends at the restricted access near the Public Transport Interchange of Lok Ma Chau Station. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. YLRM175. LOK MA CHAU ROAD A copy of Plan No. YLRM175 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Yuen Long at 8th Floor, Yuen Long Government Offices, 2 Kiu Lok Square, Yuen Long, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 24 December 2015 LEUNG Kin-wah for Director of Lands
