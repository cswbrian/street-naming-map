## English

G.N. 9867 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of UN CHAU STREET in the district of Sham Shui Po, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 3077 dated 11 May 2007 with immediate effect:— Description Name The street is approximately 1 800 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tai Po Road and ends at its junction with Castle Peak Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM52a. UN CHAU STREET A copy of Plan No. KRM52a and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 22 December 2017 LEUNG Kin-wah for Director of Lands
