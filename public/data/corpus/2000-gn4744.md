## English

G.N. 4744 LANDS DEPARTMENT STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in Kwun Tong District, Kowloon, will be known as from the date of this notice as described hereunder:— Description Name KO CHIU PATH Plan No. KRM 1 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon and in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong. 21 July 2000 K. F. YEUNG for Director of Lands The road is approximately 65 metres long. It starts at its junction with Ko Chiu Road and ends at the entrances of Buddhist Ho Nam Kam College and The Hong Kong Taoist Association Chan Lui Chung Tak Memorial School. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. KRM 1.
