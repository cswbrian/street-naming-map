## English

G.N. 995 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the Kwun Tong District, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 200 metres long. It starts from its junction with Wai Yip Street and ends at its junctions with Hoi Yuen Road and Kei Yip Lane respectively. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured blue on Plan No. KRM150. SHEUNG YIP STREET A copy of Plan No. KRM150 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the Map Publications Centre, Hong Kong at 6th Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 13 February 2026 Danny YEUNG for Director of Lands
