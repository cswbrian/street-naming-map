## English

G.N. 6972 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of PRINCE EDWARD ROAD EAST in the districts of Kowloon City and Wong Tai Sin, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 111 dated 12 January 1979 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 2 040 metres long. It starts from its junction with Prince Edward Road West and ends at its junction with Kwun Tong Road, with branches connecting Choi Hung Road and Concorde Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM114. PRINCE EDWARD ROAD EAST A copy of Plan No. KRM114 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 14 September 2018 LEUNG Kin-wah for Director of Lands
