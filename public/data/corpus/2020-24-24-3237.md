## English

G.N. 3237 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of the following road in the district of Sai Kung, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. The road is approximately 630 metres long. It starts from its junction with Hiram’s Highway and runs northwesterly for about 170 metres where it meets Ho Chung North Road. It continues to run northerly for about 460 metres to where it terminates. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. SKRM160. LUK MEI TSUEN ROAD G.N. 1786 dated 24 May 1991 A copy of Plan No. SKRM160 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Sai Kung at 3rd Floor, Sai Kung Government Offices, 34 Chan Man Street, Sai Kung, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong; or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 12 June 2020 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
