## English

G.N. 6570 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following streets in the district of Tai Po, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 415 metres long. It starts from the roundabout joining Pok Yin Road and Yau King Lane. It ends at its junction with an unnamed road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. TPRM115. CHEUNG TAI ROAD The street is approximately 42 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tai Po Tau Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. TPRM116. KA KING LANE A copy of Plan Nos. TPRM115 and TPRM116, and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Tai Po at 5th Floor, Tai Po Government Offices, 1 Ting Kok Road, Tai Po, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 1 November 2013 CHENG Wai-pun for Director of Lands
