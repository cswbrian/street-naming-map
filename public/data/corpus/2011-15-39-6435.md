## English

G.N. 6435 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of FU CHUNG LANE in the district of Tai Po, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 1435 dated 30 April 1993 with immediate effect:— Description Name The street is approximately 72 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tung Leung Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. TPRM109. FU CHUNG LANE Plan No. TPRM109 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Tai Po at 5th Floor, Tai Po Government Offices, 1 Ting Kok Road, Tai Po, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong and in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 30 September 2011 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
