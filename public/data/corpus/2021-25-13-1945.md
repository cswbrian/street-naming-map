## English

G.N. 1945 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of the following road in the district of Kowloon City, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. The road is approximately 270 metres long. It starts from its junction with Fat Kwong Street and runs northeasterly for about 120 metres where it meets an unnamed road. It continues to run northwesterly for about 150 metres and ends at a cul-de-sac. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM129. SHEUNG FOO STREET G.N. 751 dated 7 April 1967 A copy of Plan No. KRM129 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 3rd Floor, South Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 1 April 2021 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
