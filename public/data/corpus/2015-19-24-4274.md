## English

G.N. 4274 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in the district of Central and Western, Hong Kong Island as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 115 metres long. It starts from its junction with Garden Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. HKRM35. TRAMWAY LANE A copy of Plan No. HKRM35 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Hong Kong at 18th Floor, Southorn Centre, 130–150 Hennessy Road, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 12 June 2015 NG Kwok-wai for Director of Lands
