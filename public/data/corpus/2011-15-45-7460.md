## English

G.N. 7460 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in the district of Sham Shui Po, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 350 metres long. It starts from its junction with Yen Chow Street West and ends at the gate near the Cheung Sha Wan Wholesale Food Market. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured grey on Plan No. KRM70. WHOLESALE MARKET STREET Plan No. KRM70 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 11 November 2011 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
