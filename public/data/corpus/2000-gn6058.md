ISRM 51, ISRM 53 and ISRM 54 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Islands at 18th Floor, Harbour Building, 38 Pier Road, Central, Hong Kong and in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong. 15 September 2000 K. BIJOU DRIVE Road commencing from its junction with Discovery Valley Road and an unnamed road running generally in a westerly direction for about 700 metres. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown hatched black on Plan No. ISRM 53. MARINA DRIVE Road commencing from its junction with Discovery Bay Road running generally in a south-easterly direction for about 450 metres. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. ISRM 51.
