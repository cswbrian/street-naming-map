## English

G.N. 852 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in the district of Kowloon City, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 560 metres long. It starts from its junction with Hung Hom Road and ends at its junction with Fat Kwong Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. KRM51. FAT KWONG STREET FLYOVER Plan No. KRM51 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong and in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 6 February 2009 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands

## 中文
