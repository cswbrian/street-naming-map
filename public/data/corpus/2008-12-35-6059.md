## English

G.N. 6059 LANDS DEPARTMENT STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in the district of Kwun Tong, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name Plan No. KRM54a may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong and in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 29 August 2008 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands KO ON LANE The street is approximately 125 metres long. It starts from its junction with Cha Kwo Ling Road and ends at its junction with Sze Shan Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. KRM54a.

## 中文

第 6059 號公告 地政總署 街道命名 現根據 《公眾宪生及市政條例》 ( 第 132 章 ) 第 111C(1)( a ) 條公布，下列位於九龍觀塘區的街道， 由本公告日期起，採用以下名稱： 說明 名稱 查閱第 KRM54a 號圖則，可到九龍上海街 250 號油麻地停車場大廈 10 樓九龍測量處、香港 北角渣華道 333 號北角政府合署 23 樓地政總署測繪處，或香港銅鑼灣高士威道 66 號香港中央 圖書館 5 樓地圖圖書館。 2008 年 8 月 29 日 地政總署署長 ( 邵偉青代行 ) 高安里 這街道長約 125 米，以其與茶果嶺道的交界處為起 點，至其與四山街的交界處終結。位置及路線在第 KRM54a 號圖則上以黑網點標明。
