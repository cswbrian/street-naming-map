## English

G.N. 3236 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Sai Kung, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 270 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ho Chung Road and ends at its junction with Luk Mei Tsuen Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured blue on Plan No. SKRM160. HO CHUNG NORTH ROAD A copy of Plan No. SKRM160 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Sai Kung at 3rd Floor, Sai Kung Government Offices, 34 Chan Man Street, Sai Kung, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong; or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 12 June 2020 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
