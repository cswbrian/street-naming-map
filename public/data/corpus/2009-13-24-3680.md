## English

G.N. 3680 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in Tuen Mun District, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 76 metres long. It extends from the existing Lung Mun Road near Lung Mun Oasis and Glorious Garden, and ends at a roundabout. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. TMRM112. LUNG MUN ROAD Plan No. TMRM112 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Tuen Mun at 5th Floor, Tuen Mun Government Offices, 1 Tuen Hi Road, Tuen Mun, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 12 June 2009 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
