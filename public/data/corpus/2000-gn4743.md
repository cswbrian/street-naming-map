## English

G.N. 4743 LANDS DEPARTMENT STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Yuen Long, New Territories will be known as from the date of this notice as described hereunder:— Description Name FUNG CHEUNG ROAD Plan No. YLRM 104 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Yuen Long at 8th Floor, Yuen Long Government Offices and Tai Kiu Market, 2 Kiu Lok Square, Yuen Long, New Territories; and in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong. 21 July 2000 K. F. YEUNG for Director of Lands The road is approximately 230 metres long. It is an extension of the existing Fung Cheung Road and ends at the cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. YLRM 104.
