## English

G.N. 4780 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of following road in the districts of Sham Shui Po and Yau Tsim Mong, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. The road is approximately 3 700 metres long. It starts from its two junctions with Boundary Street, and ends at its two different junctions with Kwai Chung Road and an unnamed road, with branches joining Lai Chi Kok Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM125. CHEUNG SHA WAN ROAD G.N. 411 dated 28 September1923 G.N. 699 dated 25 November 1927 G.N. 2641 dated 13 October 1978 A copy of Plan No. KRM125 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 3rd Floor, South Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 26 July 2019 CHAN Siu-bun for Director of Lands
