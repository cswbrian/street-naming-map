## English

G.N. 4865 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Sai Kung, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 400 metres long. It extends from the existing Pak Shing Kok Road and ends at a cul-de-sac near Tseung Kwan O East Low Level Fresh Water Service Reservoir. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. SKRM155a. PAK SHING KOK ROAD A copy of Plan No. SKRM155a and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Sai Kung at 3rd Floor, Sai Kung Government Offices, 34 Chan Man Street, Sai Kung, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 3 July 2015 NG Kwok-wai for Director of Lands
