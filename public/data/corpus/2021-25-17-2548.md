## English

G.N. 2548 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of the following road in the Southern District, Hong Kong Island as set out hereunder will replace that set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. The road is approximately 1 170 metres long. It starts from its junction with Nam Long Shan Road, and ends at its junction with Ocean Drive. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. HKRM55. SHUM WAN ROAD G.N. 1915 dated 26 September 1969 A copy of Plan No. HKRM55 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Hong Kong at 18th Floor, Southorn Centre, 130–150 Hennessy Road, Wan Chai, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 30 April 2021 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
