## English

G.N. 6836 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of the following road in the Sai Kung District, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace those set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. The road is approximately 460 metres long. It starts from its junction with Wan Po Road, and ends at its junction with Tseung Lam Highway. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. SKRM164. WAN O ROAD G.N. 935 dated 14 February 2003 A copy of Plan No. SKRM164 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the District Survey Office, Sai Kung at 3rd Floor, Sai Kung Government Offices, 34 Chan Man Street, Sai Kung, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 25 November 2022 CHU Siu-ki for Director of Lands
