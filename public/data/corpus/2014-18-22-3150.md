## English

G.N. 3150 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following streets in the district of Islands, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 84 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ying Tung Road and ends at a cul-de-sac. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. ISRM91. YING HONG STREET The road is approximately 205 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ying Hei Road and ends at its junction with Ying Hong Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured green on Plan No. ISRM91. YING TUNG ROAD A copy of Plan No. ISRM91 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Islands at 18th Floor, Harbour Building, 38 Pier Road, Central, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 30 May 2014 NG Kwok-wai for Director of Lands
