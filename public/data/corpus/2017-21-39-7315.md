## English

G.N. 7315 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the descriptions of WING TUNG STREET and YAU WING STREET in the district of Islands, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 1753 dated 3 June 1983 and G.N. 3454 dated 18 October 1985 respectively with immediate effect:— Description Name The street is approximately 330 metres long. It starts from a cul-de- sac at the east. It ends at its junction with Yau Wing Street at the west. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. ISRM97. WING TUNG STREET The street is approximately 180 metres long. It starts from its junction with Wing Tung Street. It ends at a cul-de-sac near Tung Wan Barbecue Area Peng Chau. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured green on Plan No. ISRM97. YAU WING STREET A copy of Plan No. ISRM97 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Islands at 18th Floor, Harbour Building, 38 Pier Road, Central, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 29 September 2017 LEUNG Kin-wah for Director of Lands
