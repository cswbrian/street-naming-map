ROAD TRAFFIC (ROADS AND SIGNS) REGULATIONS (Chapter 220)
In exercise of the powers vested in me under regulation 18A(1)(b) of the Road Traffic
(Roads and Signs) Regulations, I hereby direct that with effect from 10.00 a.m. on |
10 May 1979, the existing 7.00 a.m. to 10.00 a.m. and 4.00 p.m. to 7.00 p.m. restricted
zones restriction on the following roads will be rescinded : — .
(1) the section of the eastbound carriageway of Castle Peak Road, Tsuen Wan :
between the points about 195 metres and 65 metres west of its junction with |
Route Twisk; and
(2) the section of the eastbound carriageway of Castle Peak Road, Tsuen Wan
between the points about 105 metres and 150 metres east of its junction with
