## English

G.N. 1670 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of following road in the district of Yuen Long, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. The road is approximately 620 metres long. It starts near the latrine beside the nullah in To Uk Tsuen running westerly for about 360 metres where it joins with Ko Sheung Road at the north of nullah. It continues to run westerly from the other side of Ko Sheung Road at the south of nullah for about 260 metres and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. YLRM214. NAM HING WEST ROAD G.N. 3286 dated 16 May 2003 A copy of Plan No. YLRM214 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Yuen Long at 8th Floor, Yuen Long Government Offices, 2 Kiu Lok Square, Yuen Long, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 3 April 2020 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
