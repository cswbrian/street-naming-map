G.N. 512 TRANSPORT DEPARTMENT
ROAD TRAFFIC (ROADS AND SIGNS) REGULATIONS (Chapter 220)
In exercise of the powers vested in me under regulation 18A(1)(a) of the Road Traffic
(Roads and Signs) Regulations, I hereby direct that with effect from 10.00 a.m. on
20 February 1980 public light buses will be prohibited from entering Siu Lek Yuen
Road, Sha Tin unless authorized in writing by the Commissioner for Transport.
Appropriate traffic signs will be erected to guide motorists.
