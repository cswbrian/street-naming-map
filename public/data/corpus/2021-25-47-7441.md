## English

G.N. 7441 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the Kwun Tong District, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 328 metres long. It starts from its junction with Sin Fat Road, and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM131. KO LING ROAD A copy of Plan No. KRM131 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 3rd Floor, South Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 26 November 2021 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
