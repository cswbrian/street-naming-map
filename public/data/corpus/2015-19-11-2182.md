## English

G.N. 2182 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in the district of Yuen Long, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 415 metres long. It starts from its junction with Hong Yip Street and ends at its junction with Tak Yip Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. YLRM173. LAU YIP STREET A copy of Plan No. YLRM173 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Yuen Long at 8th Floor, Yuen Long Government Offices, 2 Kiu Lok Square, Yuen Long, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 13 March 2015 NG Kwok-wai for Director of Lands
