## English

G.N. 4729 LANDS DEPARTMENT STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following roads in the district of North, New Territories as described hereunder will be known as from the date of this notice:— Description Name Plan No. DNRM84c may be inspected in the District Survey Office, North, at 5th Floor, North District Government Offices, Fanling, New Territories; and in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department, at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong. 11 July 2003 P. K. AU YEUNG for Director of Lands CHING SHING ROAD The road is approximately 195 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ching Hiu Road and ends at the cul-de-sac. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown hatched black on Plan No. DNRM84c. CHING HIU ROAD The road is approximately 580 metres long. It starts from its junction with Pak Wo Road and ends at its junction with Po Kin Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. DNRM84c.
