## English

G.N. 4022 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of YAU TIN WEST ROAD in the district of Yuen Long, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace those set out in G.N. 4153 dated 20 November 1992 and G.N. 4723 dated 9 December 1994 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 600 metres long. It starts from its junction with Yuen Lung Street and ends at its junction with Long Ho Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. YLRM172. YAU TIN WEST ROAD A copy of Plan No. YLRM172 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Yuen Long at 8th Floor, Yuen Long Government Offices, 2 Kiu Lok Square, Yuen Long, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 11 July 2014 NG Kwok-wai for Director of Lands
