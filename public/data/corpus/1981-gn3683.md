ROAD TRAFFIC (ROADS AND SIGNS) REGULATIONS (Chapter 220)

In exercise of the powers vested in me under regulation 18A(1)(a) of the Road Traffic
(Roads' and Signs) Regulations, Chapter 220, I hereby direct that with effect from
7.00 a.m. on 13 December 1981, the section of the traffic lane on the westbound tram

track of King’s Road between a point approximately 50 meters :east of its junction with
Healthy Street Central and its junction with Tin Chiu Street will be designated as
a prohibited zone. All westbound motor vehicles except trams will be prohibited from,
entering the prohibited zone 24 hours daily. > '
The extent of the prohibited zone will be indicated by the appropriate traffic signs ~
and carriageway markings. .
