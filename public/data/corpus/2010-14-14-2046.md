## English

G.N. 2046 Lands Department STREET NAMES Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following streets in the district of Sha Tin, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 1 110 metres long. It starts from its junction with Shui Chuen Au Street and ends at a cul-de-sac near Shui Chuen O Fresh Water Service Reservoir. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. STRM1153b. POK CHUEN STREET The street is approximately 140 metres long. It starts from its junction with Pok Chuen Street and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured yellow on Plan No. STRM1153b. POK CHUEN PATH The street is approximately 700 metres long. It extends from the exiting To Shek Street and ends at its junction with Pok Chuen Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. STRM1154a. TO SHEK STREET The street is approximately 155 metres long. It starts from its junction with To Shek Street and ends at a cul-de-sac near To Shek Fresh Water Pumping Station. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured blue on Plan No. STRM1154a. TO SHEK PATH Plan Nos. STRM1153b and STRM1154a may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Sha Tin at 12th Floor, Sha Tin Government Offices, 1 Sheung Wo Che Road, Sha Tin, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 9 April 2010 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
