## English

G.N. 851 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in Southern District, Hong Kong as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 235 metres long. It is an extension of the existing Bel-Air Peak Avenue. It starts from the roundabout of the existing Bel-Air Peak Avenue and ends at a cul-de-sac at the north. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. HKRM21a. BEL-AIR PEAK AVENUE Plan No. HKRM21a may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Hong Kong at 18th Floor, Southorn Centre, 130–150 Hennessy Road, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong and in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 6 February 2009 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
