## English

G.N. 933 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of SAI KUNG RURAL COMMITTEE LANE in the district of Sai Kung, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 1271 dated 10 April 1992 with immediate effect:— Description Name The street is approximately 62 metres long. It starts from its junction with Hiram’s Highway and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. SKRM86b. SAI KUNG RURAL COMMITTEE LANE A copy of Plan No. SKRM86b and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Sai Kung at 3rd Floor, Sai Kung Government Offices, 34 Chan Man Street, Sai Kung, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 14 February 2014 NG Kwok-wai for Director of Lands
