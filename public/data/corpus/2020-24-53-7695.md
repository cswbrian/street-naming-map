## English

G.N. 7695 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Tuen Mun, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The interchange connecting Tsing Tin Road and Tuen Mun Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. TMRM128. TSING TIN INTERCHANGE A copy of Plan No. TMRM128 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Tuen Mun at 5th Floor, Tuen Mun Government Offices, 1 Tuen Hi Road, Tuen Mun, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 31 December 2020 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
