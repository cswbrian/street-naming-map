## English

G.N. 4509 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of following road in the district of Kwun Tong, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. The road is approximately 42 metres long. It starts from its junction with Cha Kwo Ling Road and ends at where it connects with Fan Wa Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured yellow on Plan No. KRM124. WING FOOK STREET G.N. 1982 dated 2 August 1974 A copy of Plan No. KRM124 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 3/F, South Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 7 August 2020 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
