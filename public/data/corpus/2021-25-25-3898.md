## English

G.N. 3898 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of the following roads in the Kowloon City District and Kwun Tong District, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace those set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. The road is approximately 600 metres long. It starts from a cul-de-sac in the south, and ends at its junction with Sheung Yee Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured yellow on Plan No. KRM133. CHEUNG YIP STREET G.N. 2898 dated 9 November 1979, G.N. 1193 dated 19 April 1984 and G.N. 3136 dated 26 October 1984 The road is approximately 340 metres long. It starts from its junction with Cheung Yip Street, and ends at its junction with Kai Tak Bridge Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM133.
