## English

G.N. 1161 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in Tuen Mun District, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 1 000 metres long. It starts from its junction with Castle Peak Road—Castle Peak Bay. It ends at an unnamed road near the Crossroads Foundation. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. TMRM120c. TSING YING ROAD A copy of Plan No. TMRM120c and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Tuen Mun at 5th Floor, Tuen Mun Government Offices, 1 Tuen Hi Road, Tuen Mun, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 1 March 2013 CHENG Wai-pun for Director of Lands
