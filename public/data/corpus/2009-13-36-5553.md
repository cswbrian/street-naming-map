## English

G.N. 5553 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of MEI TIN ROAD in the district of Sha Tin, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 1737 dated 15 June 1984 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 1 930 metres long. It starts from its junction with Che Kung Miu Road and ends at its junction with Tung Lo Wan Hill Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. STRM1148e. MEI TIN ROAD Plan No. STRM1148e may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Sha Tin at 12th Floor, Sha Tin Government Offices, 1 Sheung Wo Che Road, Sha Tin, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 4 September 2009 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
