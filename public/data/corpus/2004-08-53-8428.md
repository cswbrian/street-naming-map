## English

G.N. 8428 LANDS DEPARTMENT
STREET NAME

Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services

Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Yuen Long, New Territories as

described hereunder will be known as from the date of this notice:—

Description Name

The road is approximately 1 170 metres long. It starts from the KAM TIN BYPASS

roundabout at Kam Tin Road and Kam Ho Road. It ends at the

roundabout at Kam Tin Road, Shek Kong Airfield Road and

Tung Wui Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly

as shown coloured yellow on Plan No. YLRM131.

Plan No. YLRM131 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Yuen Long at 8th Floor,
Yuen Long Government Offices, 2 Kiu Lok Square, Yuen Long, New Territories; in the Survey
and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java
Road, North Point, Hong Kong and in the Map Library at Sth Floor, Hong Kong Central
Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong.

31 December 2004 MAN Wing-kan for Director of Lands
