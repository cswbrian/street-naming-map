## English

G.N. 377 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following roads in the Yuen Long District, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 1 490 metres long. It starts from its junction with Kwu Tung Road and ends at its junction with Ma Tso Lung Road, with branches connecting Heung Tsz Road and Kwu Chun Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. YLRM223. KWU TUNG NORTH ROAD The road is approximately 330 metres long. It starts from its junction with Kwu Tung North Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured yellow on Plan No. YLRM223. KWU CHUN STREET A copy of Plan No. YLRM223 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the District Survey Office, Yuen Long at No. 2 Yuen Ching Road, Yuen Long, New Territories; in the Map Publications Centre, Hong Kong at 6th Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 16 January 2026 Danny YEUNG for Director of Lands
