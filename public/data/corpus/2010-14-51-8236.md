## English

G.N. 8236 Lands Department DECLARATION TO CHANGE STREET NAME With reference to G.N. 5531 dated 27 August 2010, it is hereby declared under section 111C(1)( b ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that FO KING ROAD in the district of Tai Po, New Territories is renamed as FO CHUN ROAD as described hereunder from the date of this notice:— Description Name The road is approximately 840 metres long. It starts from its junction with Chong San Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. TPRM107. FO CHUN ROAD Plan No. TPRM107 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Tai Po at 5th Floor, Tai Po Government Offices, 1 Ting Kok Road, Tai Po, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 24 December 2010 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
