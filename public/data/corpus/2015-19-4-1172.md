## English

G.N. 1172 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of CONCORDE ROAD in the district of Kowloon City, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 3682 dated 11 December 1981 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 840 metres long. It starts from the roundabout near the Kowloon City No. 1 Sewage Pumping Station. It ends at the roundabout joining Shing Kai Road and Muk Chun Street. Its location and alignment are more particular as shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM78. CONCORDE ROAD A copy of Plan No. KRM78 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 23 January 2015 NG Kwok-wai for Director of Lands
