## English

G.N. 6314 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of the following road in the Yuen Long District, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. The road is approximately 330 metres long. It starts from its junction with Yuen Long On Ning Road, and ends at its junctions with Sai Tai Street, Fook Hong Street and Castle Peak Road—Yuen Long respectively. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. YLRM205. YUEN LONG PAU CHEUNG SQUARE G.N. 2944 dated 2 December 1977 A copy of Plan No. YLRM205 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the District Survey Office, Yuen Long at 8th Floor, Yuen Long Government Offices, 2 Kiu Lok Square, Yuen Long, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 8 October 2021 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
