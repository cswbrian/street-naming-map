## English

G.N. 1314 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of LUNG WO ROAD in Central and Western District, Hong Kong Island as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 7642 dated 4 December 2009 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 1 050 metres long. It starts from its junction with Man Yiu Street and ends at its junction with Lung Wui Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. HKRM34. LUNG WO ROAD Plan No. HKRM34 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Hong Kong at 18th Floor, Southorn Centre, 130–150 Hennessy Road, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 25 February 2011 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
