## English

G.N. 2869 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Sha Tin, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 205 metres long. It extends from the existing Yiu Sha Road, and ends at its junction with an unnamed road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. STRM1164b. YIU SHA ROAD A copy of Plan No. STRM1164b and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Sha Tin at 12th Floor, Sha Tin Government Offices, 1 Sheung Wo Che Road, Sha Tin, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 26 April 2019 CHAN Siu-bun for Director of Lands
