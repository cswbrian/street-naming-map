ROAD TRAFFIC (ROADS AND SIGNS) REGULATIONS (Chapter 220)
/
TRAM ONLY LANE—KING’S ROAD
In exercise of the powers vested in me under regulation 18A(1)(a) of the Road Traffic (Roads and;
Signs) Regulations, Chapter 220, I hereby direct that with effect from 10.00 a.m. on 19 April 1984,
the section of the traffic lane on the eastbound tram track of King’s Road between House No. 501
and its junction with Tin Chiu Street will be designated a prohibited zone. All eastbound motor
vehicles will be prohibited from entering the prohibited zone 24 hours daily.
The extent of the prohibited zone will be indicated by appropriate traffic signs and carriageway
markings.
