## English

G.N. 4507 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Tai Po, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 265 metres long. It is an extension of existing Tung Cheong Street. It starts from its junction with Plover Cove Road, and ends at its junction with Nam Wan Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. TPRM122b. TUNG CHEONG STREET A copy of Plan No. TPRM122b and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Tai Po at 5th Floor, Tai Po Government Offices, 1 Ting Kok Road, Tai Po, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 7 August 2020 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
