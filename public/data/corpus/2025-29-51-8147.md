## English

G.N. 8147 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of the following road in the Yau Tsim Mong District, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace those set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. The road is approximately 475 metres long. Its starts from its junction with Museum Drive and ends at a roundabout connecting three proposed roads. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM149. CULTURAL DRIVE G.N. 5801 dated 17 September 2021 A copy of Plan No. KRM149 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the Map Publications Centre, Hong Kong at 6th Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 19 December 2025 Danny YEUNG for Director of Lands
