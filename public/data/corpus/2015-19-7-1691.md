## English

G.N. 1691 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in the district of Kowloon City, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 53 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ko Shan Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. KRM79. WAI YIN PATH A copy of Plan No. KRM79 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 13 February 2015 NG Kwok-wai for Director of Lands
