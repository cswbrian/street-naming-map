## English

G.N. 5983 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Yau Tsim Mong, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 1 550 metres long. It starts from its junctions with Gascoigne Road and Chatham Road South, and ends at its junctions with Ferry Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM39. GASCOIGNE ROAD FYLOVER A copy of Plan No. KRM39 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 3rd Floor, South Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 16 October 2020 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
