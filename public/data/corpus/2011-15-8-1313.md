## English

G.N. 1313 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in Central and Western District, Hong Kong Island as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 115 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tim Mei Avenue and Lung Wui Road. It ends at its junction with Lung Wo Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured green on Plan No. HKRM34. LEGISLATIVE COUNCIL ROAD Plan No. HKRM34 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Hong Kong at 18th Floor, Southorn Centre, 130–150 Hennessy Road, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 25 February 2011 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
