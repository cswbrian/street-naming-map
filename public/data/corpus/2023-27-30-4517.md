## English

G.N. 4517 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the Kwun Tong District, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 100 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tak Tin Street, and ends at its junction with On Tin Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM142. CHI TIN STREET A copy of Plan No. KRM142 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 3rd Floor, South Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon; in the Map Publications Centre, Hong Kong at 6th Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 28 July 2023 CHU Siu-ki for Director of Lands
