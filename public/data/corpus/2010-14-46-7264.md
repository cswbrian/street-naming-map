## English

G.N. 7264 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of LUNG HONG STREET in the district of Tuen Mun, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 5851 dated 21 September 2001 with immediate effect:— Description Name The street is approximately 270 metres long. It starts from its junction with Lung Yiu Street and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. TMRM115c. LUNG HONG STREET Plan No. TMRM115c may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Tuen Mun at 5th Floor, Tuen Mun Government Offices, 1 Tuen Hi Road, Tuen Mun, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 19 November 2010 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
