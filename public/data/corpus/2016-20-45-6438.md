## English

G.N. 6438 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in the district of Islands, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 270 metres long. It extends from the existing Ying Tung Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown stippled black on Plan No. ISRM94. YING TUNG ROAD A copy of Plan No. ISRM94 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Islands at 18th Floor, Harbour Building, 38 Pier Road, Central, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 11 November 2016 LEUNG Kin-wah for Director of Lands
