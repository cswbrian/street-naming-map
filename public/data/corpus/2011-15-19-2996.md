## English

G.N. 2996 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following streets in the district of Sha Tin, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 165 metres long. It starts from its junction with Mei Tin Road and ends at its junction with Heung Fan Liu Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. STRM1156a. MEI FAI STREET The street is approximately 74 metres long. It starts from the roundabout joining Chik Wan Street, Lower Shing Mun Road and Tai Po Road—Tai Wai. It ends at the entrance to a car park. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. STRM1157a. YING WAN LANE Plan Nos. STRM1156a and STRM1157a may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Sha Tin at 12th Floor, Sha Tin Government Offices, 1 Sheung Wo Che Road, Sha Tin, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 13 May 2011 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
