G.N. 3528 TRANSPORT DEPARTMENT
ROAD TRAFFIC (TRAFFIC CONTROL) REGULATIONS (Chapter 374)
RESTRICTED ZONES IN TSUEN WAN

In exercise of the powers vested in me under regulation 14(1)() of the Road Traffic (Traffic Control)
Regulations, Chapter 374, I hereby direct that with effect from 10.00 a.m. on Friday, 30 November
1984, the following roads will be designated as 24-hours restricted zones to all vehicles except those
with permits issued by the Commissioner for Transport:—

(a) The eastbound carriageway of Tsuen Wan Market Street between Yan Chai Street and Kwan

Mun Hau Street;
(b) The southbound carriageway of Kwan Mun Hau Street between Tsuen Wan Street and Tak
Wah Street;

(c) The northbound carriageway of Kwan Mun Hau Street between Tsuen Wan Market Street

and Tsuen Wah Street;

(d) The northbound carriageway of Kwan Mun Hau Street between Tak Wah Street and Tsuen

Lok Street;

(e) The northern kerbside lane of Tak Wah Street;

(f)A short section of about 10 metres of the southern kerbside lane of Tak Wah Street at its

junction with Kwan Mun Hau Street;

(g) The eastern kerbside lane of Ham Tin Street north of its junction with Sha Tsui Road; and

(h) Yan Chai Street between Tsuen Wan Market Street and Tak Wah Street; excepting part of

Yan Chai Street, about 83 metres, which is closed to all vehicular traffic.

Within the restricted zones, all vehicles except those with permits issued by the Commissioner for
Transport will be prohibited from:—

(a) picking up or setting down passengers; or

(b) loading or unloading goods.

The extent of the restricted zones will be indicated by appropriate traffic signs.

At the same time, the existing 24-hour restricted zone for public light buses and taxis on Ham Tin
Street between Tak Wah Street and Sha Tsui Road as gazetted under G.N. 1435/80 and f
G.N. 2323/83 respectively will be rescinded. . |

P. F. LEEDS Commissioner for Transport
