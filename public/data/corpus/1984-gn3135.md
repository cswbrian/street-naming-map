G.N. 3133 TRANSPORT DEPARTMENT
ROAD TRAFFIC (TRAFFIC CONTROL) REGULATIONS (Chapter 374)
RESTRICTED ZONES IN WANG TAU HOM AREA
In exercise of the powers vested in me under regulation 14(1)(4) of the Road Traffic (Traffic Control
Regulations, Chapter 374, I hereby direct that with effect from 10.00 a.m. on 30 October 1984, ie
following sections of roads will be designated as restricted zones from 7.00 a.m. to 10.00 a.m. and
» from 4.00 p.m. to 7.00 p.m. daily:—
(i) Wang Tau Hom East Road section between Fu Mei Street and Junction Road;
(ii) Fu Mei Street East section between its junction with Wang Tau Hom East Road and a point
approximately 30 metres south of the same junction; and
(iii) Wang Tau Hom Central Road section between its junction with Wang Tau Hom East Road
and a point approximately 30 metres west of the same junction.
Within the above restricted zones, all motor vehicles except those with permits issued by the
Commissioner for Transport will be prohibited from:—
(a) picking up or setting down passengers; or
(b) loading or unloading goods.
The extent of the restricted zones will be indicated by appropriate traffic signs.
P. F. LEEDS Commissioner for Transport
