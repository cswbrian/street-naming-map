## English

G.N. 1563 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following streets in the district of Islands, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 640 metres long. It starts from its junction with Lung Tin Street, loops clockwise at about one-third of its length and ends where it rejoins itself. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. ISRM89. LUNG SHING STREET The street is approximately 130 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tai O Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured green on Plan No. ISRM89. LUNG TIN STREET The road is approximately 275 metres long. It starts from its junction with Yung Shue Wan Main Street and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. ISRM88. YUNG SHUE WAN PLAZA ROAD A copy of Plan Nos. ISRM89 and ISRM88, and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Islands at 18th Floor, Harbour Building, 38 Pier Road, Central, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong and in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 14 March 2014 NG Kwok-wai for Director of Lands
