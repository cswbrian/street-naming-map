## English

G.N. 2547 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of the following road in the Southern District, Hong Kong Island as set out hereunder will replace those set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. The road is approximately 950 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ocean Park Road, and ends at its junction with an unnamed road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. HKRM59. HEUNG YIP ROAD G.N. 2466 dated 29 September 1972 G.N. 1383 dated 31 May 1974 G.N. 351 dated 7 February 1975 A copy of Plan No. HKRM59 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the Map Publications Centre, Hong Kong at 6th Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 25 April 2025 CHU Siu-ki for Director of Lands
