## English

G.N. 1597 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of LUNG PING ROAD in the district of Sham Shui Po, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 3803 dated 15 November 1985; and the description of NAM CHEONG STREET in the district of Sham Shui Po, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 616 dated 14 April 1961 and G.N. 1480 dated 17 July 1970 respectively with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 520 metres long. It starts from its junction with Lung Yuet Road. It ends at its junction with Nam Cheong Street and Yin Ping Road, with a branch ends at a cul-de-sac near Lung Cheung Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured blue on Plan No. KRM67. LUNG PING ROAD The street is approximately 2 740 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tung Chau Street and Boundary Street. It ends at its junction with Lung Cheung Road and another junction with Yin Ping Road and Lung Ping Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM67. NAM CHEONG STREET A copy of Plan No. KRM67 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 18 March 2016 LEUNG Kin-wah for Director of Lands
