## English

G.N. 3235 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of the following road in the district of Kowloon City, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace that set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. The road is approximately 1 250 metres long. It starts from a roundabout, and ends at the Kai Tak Cruise Terminal. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM128. SHING FUNG ROAD G.N. 6493 dated 28 September 2012 A copy of Plan No. KRM128 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 3/F, South Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong; or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 12 June 2020 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
