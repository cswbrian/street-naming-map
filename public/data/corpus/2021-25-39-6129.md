## English

G.N. 6129 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the Tai Po District, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 470 metres long. It starts from its junction with Chuen On Road, and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. TPRM127. CHOI TIP STREET A copy of Plan No. TPRM127 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the District Survey Office, Tai Po at 5th Floor, Tai Po Government Offices, 1 Ting Kok Road, Tai Po, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 30 September 2021 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
