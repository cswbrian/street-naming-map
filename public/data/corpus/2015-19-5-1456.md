## English

G.N. 1456 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of LAM TSUEN HEUNG KUNG SHO ROAD in the district of Tai Po, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 2894 dated 2 May 2008 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 485 metres long. It starts from its junction with Lam Kam Road near Chung Uk Tsuen and ends at another junction with Lam Kam Road near Hang Ha Po. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. TPRM117. LAM TSUEN HEUNG KUNG SHO ROAD A copy of Plan No. TPRM117 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Tai Po at 5th Floor, Tai Po Government Offices, 1 Ting Kok Road, Tai Po, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 30 January 2015 NG Kwok-wai for Director of Lands
