## English

G.N. 2871 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of North, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 145 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ping Che Road, and ends at its junction with an unnamed road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. DNRM102. HUNG LENG NORTH ROAD A copy of Plan No. DNRM102 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, North at 5th Floor, North District Government Offices, 3 Pik Fung Road, Fanling, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 26 April 2019 CHAN Siu-bun for Director of Lands
