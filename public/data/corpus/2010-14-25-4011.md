## English

G.N. 4011 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of North, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 740 metres long. It starts from its junction with Ma Tso Lung Road and ends at Liu Pok. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. DNRM99b. LIU POK ROAD Plan No. DNRM99b may be inspected in the District Survey Office, North at 5th Floor, North District Government Offices, Fanling, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 25 June 2010 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands

## 中文
