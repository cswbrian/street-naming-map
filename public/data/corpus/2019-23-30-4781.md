## English

G.N. 4781 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of following roads in the district of Islands, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. The road is approximately 2 960 metres long. It starts from its junction with North Lantau Highway, and ends at Airport North Interchange, with branches connecting Chun Wan Road Interchange, East Coast Road, Airport South Interchange and Cheong Hong Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured orange on Plan No. ISRM98a. AIRPORT ROAD G.N. 73 Dated 2 January 1998 The road is approximately 1 860 metres long. It starts from its junction with Airport Road, and ends at Airport North Interchange. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured red on Plan No. ISRM98a. CHEONG HONG ROAD G.N. 73 dated 2 January 1998 The road is approximately 2 300 metres long. It starts from its junction with Scenic Road, and ends at Sky City Interchange. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured green on Plan No. ISRM98a. EAST COAST ROAD G.N. 5705 dated 31 August 2007 The road is approximately 1 610 metres long. It starts from its junction with Scenic Road, and ends at its junction with Tung Fai Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. ISRM98a. KWO LO WAN ROAD G.N. 73 dated 2 January 1998 The road is approximately 480 metres long. It starts from its junction with East Coast Road, and ends at its junction with Kwo Lo Wan Road at the west and the cul-de-sac at the south. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured blue on Plan No. ISRM98a. TUNG FAI ROAD G.N. 6283 dated 18 December 1998 A copy of Plan No. ISRM98a and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Islands at 18th Floor, Harbour Building, 38 Pier Road, Central, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 26 July 2019 CHAN Siu-bun for Director of Lands
