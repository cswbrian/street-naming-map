## English

G.N. 3561 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Yau Tsim Mong, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 170 metres long. It starts from its junction with Austin Road West and ends at its junction with Wui Cheung Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. KRM64. WUI MAN ROAD Plan No. KRM64 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 5 June 2009 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
