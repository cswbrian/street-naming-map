## English

G.N. 3021 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in the district of Sai Kung, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 72 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tai Mong Tsai Road and ends at the village access near Sha Ha. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. SKRM157. SHA HA PATH A copy of Plan No. SKRM157 and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Sai Kung at 3rd Floor, Sai Kung Government Offices, 34 Chan Man Street, Sai Kung, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 27 May 2016 LEUNG Kin-wah for Director of Lands
