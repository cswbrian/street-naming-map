## English

G.N. 4562 Lands Department STREET NAMES Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following streets in the district of Kwun Tong, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 640 metres long. It extends from the existing Choi Hing Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown in pattern of black circles on Plan No. KRM69. CHOI HING ROAD The road is approximately 910 metres long. It extends from the existing Choi Wing Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. KRM69. CHOI WING ROAD The street is approximately 180 metres long. It starts from its junction with Choi Wing Road and ends at a cul-de-sac . Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown cross- hatched black on Plan No. KRM69. CHOI WING LANE Plan No. KRM69 may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 10th Floor, Yau Ma Tei Car Park Building, 250 Shanghai Street, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 16 July 2010 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands

## 中文

第 4562 號公告 地政總署 街道命名 現根據 《公眾衞生及市政條例》 ( 第 132 章 ) 第 111C(1)( a ) 條公布，下述位於九龍觀塘區的街道， 由本公告日期起，採用以下名稱： 說明 名稱 這道路長約 640 米，由現有彩興路延伸出來，至其盡頭處終結。 位置及路線在第 KRM69 號圖則上以黑圓形圖案標明。 彩興路 這道路長約 910 米，由現有彩榮路延伸出來，至其盡頭處終結。 位置及路線在第 KRM69 號圖則上以黑網點標明。 彩榮路 這街道長約 180 米，以其與彩榮路的交界處為起點，至其盡頭處 終結。位置及路線在第 KRM69 號圖則上以黑交叉線標明。 彩榮里 查閱第 KRM69 號圖則，可到九龍上海街 250 號油麻地停車場大廈 10 樓九龍測量處、香港 北角渣華道 333 號北角政府合署 23 樓地政總署測繪處，或香港銅鑼灣高士威道 66 號香港中央 圖書館 5 樓地圖圖書館。 2010 年 7 月 16 日 地政總署署長 ( 邵偉青代行 )
