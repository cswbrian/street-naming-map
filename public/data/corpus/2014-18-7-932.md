## English

G.N. 932 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Kwai Tsing, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 290 metres long. It starts from its junction with Cheung Hang Road at the east and ends at another junction with Cheung Hang Road at the north. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. KTRM77a. TAI CHING CHEUNG ROAD A copy of Plan No. KTRM77a and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Tsuen Wan and Kwai Tsing at 15th Floor, Tsuen Wan Government Offices, 38 Sai Lau Kok Road, Tsuen Wan, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 14 February 2014 NG Kwok-wai for Director of Lands
