## English

G.N. 4533 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in Tuen Mun District, New Territories as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 420 metres long. It starts from its junction with Lung Mun Road and ends at its junction with Yip Wong Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. TMRM114c. LUNG CHAK ROAD A copy of Plan No. TMRM114c and this notice may be inspected on the Lands Department website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Tuen Mun at 5th Floor, Tuen Mun Government Offices, 1 Tuen Hi Road, Tuen Mun, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 2 August 2013 CHENG Wai-pun for Director of Lands
