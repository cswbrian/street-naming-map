## English

G.N. 5350 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)( a ) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following street in the district of Central and Western, Hong Kong Island as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The street is approximately 45 metres long. It is an extension of the existing Lung Wah Street. It starts from its junction with Smithfield and joins the existing Lung Wah Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown stippled black on Plan No. HKRM26a. LUNG WAH STREET Plan No. HKRM26a may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Hong Kong at 18th Floor, Southorn Centre, 130–150 Hennessy Road, Hong Kong; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 20 August 2010 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
