## English

G.N. 996 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given that the description of the following roads in the Kwun Tong District, Kowloon as set out hereunder will replace those set out in the previous G.N. with immediate effect:— Description Name Previous G.N. The road is approximately 600 metres long. It starts from its junctions with Sheung Yip Street and Wai Yip Street respectively, and ends at its junction with Kwun Tong Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM150. HOI YUEN ROAD G.N. 264 dated 22 February 1957 The road is approximately 110 metres long. It starts from its junction with Kei Yip Street and ends at its junction with Sheung Yip Street. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured yellow on Plan No. KRM150. KEI YIP LANE G.N. 310 dated 27 January 1989 A copy of Plan No. KRM150 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices.html) under Government Notices of Resources page; in the Map Publications Centre, Hong Kong at 6th Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 13 February 2026 Danny YEUNG for Director of Lands
