## English

G.N. 6775 Lands Department STREET NAME Notice is hereby given under section 111C(1)(a) of the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance, Chapter 132, that the following road in the district of Kwun Tong, Kowloon as described hereunder will be known from the date of this notice as:— Description Name The road is approximately 5 500 metres long. It starts from its junctions with Lei Yue Mun Road and Tseung Kwan O Road respectively, and ends at its junctions with Prince Edward Road East and an unnamed road respectively, with branches connecting Wai Fat Road, Wai Yip Street, Wang Chiu Road and Kai Fuk Road. Its location and alignment are more particularly shown coloured pink on Plan No. KRM38. KWUN TONG BYPASS A copy of Plan No. KRM38 and this notice can be inspected on the Lands Department’s website (www.landsd.gov.hk) under Government Notices; in the District Survey Office, Kowloon at 3rd Floor, South Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Kowloon; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 20 November 2020 CHANG Kwok-fai for Director of Lands
