## English

G.N. 7994 Lands Department STREET NAMES Notice is hereby given that the descriptions of LAI PING ROAD and YUNG PING PATH in the Sha Tin District, New Territories as set out hereunder will replace that set out in G.N. 3532 dated 30 November 1984 and G.N. 4337 dated 4 December 1992 with immediate effect:— Description Name The road is approximately 2 110 metres long. It starts from its junction with Tai Po Road—Ma Liu Shui and ends at a cul-de-sac at the northwest. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured pink on Plan No. STRM1149b. The street is approximately 350 metres long. It starts from its junction with Lai Ping Road and ends at a cul-de-sac at the west. Its location and alignment are more particularly as shown coloured yellow on Plan No. STRM1149b. LAI PING ROAD YUNG PING PATH Plan No. STRM1149b may be inspected in the District Survey Office, Sha Tin at 12th Floor, Sha Tin Government Offices, 1 Sheung Wo Che Road, Sha Tin, New Territories; in the Survey and Mapping Office, Lands Department at 23rd Floor, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point, Hong Kong or in the Map Library at 5th Floor, Hong Kong Central Library, 66 Causeway Road, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. 18 December 2009 SIU Wai-ching for Director of Lands
